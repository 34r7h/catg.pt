# Boop.me — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
- **For organizers:** "The event app without the app" — registration/ticketing on your own domain, agenda and calendar invites, QR check-in and badge printing, curated matchmaking, SMS updates, post-event sponsor reporting: "sell the seats, book the meetings, prove the room worked."
- **For exhibitors/sales teams:** QR-to-SMS lead capture that books the meeting before the visitor leaves the booth — "they capture leads, we book meetings." Talking point: "lead capture fills your CRM. Boop fills your calendar."
- **For attendees/individuals:** scan someone's QR, their contact card arrives by text (.vcf), meetings book inside the SMS thread — free to start, no app needed on either side.

## How it works (the core flow)
1. Visitor scans a QR (booth, badge, or personal) → their native Messages app opens with a pre-written SMS, one tap to send (US codes go to **+1 866 395 7922**; UK number and WhatsApp option exist).
2. Boop replies in the thread: sends the contact card (.vcf), asks qualifying questions (custom flow per QR/rep), offers real slots read live from the rep's Google/Outlook calendar.
3. Prospect replies with a number → booking written to the rep's calendar, invite sent to both (Google Meet/Zoom link auto-added); SMS reminders at 24h and 30 min.
4. Contact auto-enriches (email or LinkedIn URL → title, company, industry), syncs to CRM (HubSpot automatic, Salesforce one-click Leads, Zoho; CSV/webhooks anytime).
5. Everything runs over the cellular network — no app install, no venue wifi, works offline for QR/SMS.
