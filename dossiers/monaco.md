## 1. What it is
**Monaco is an AI-native sales platform for early-stage startups, aiming to replace the traditional CRM and the patchwork of sales tools.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #37.
- **Co-founders, per Monaco:** Sam Blond (formerly partner at Founders Fund and CRO at Brex) and his brother Brian Blond, with Abishek Viswanathan (formerly CPO at Apollo and Qualtrics) and Malay Desai (formerly SVP of engineering at Clari).
- **Starting observation, per the founders:** many promising startups struggle or fail early because they do not understand sales and cannot generate repeatable revenue.

## 2. What the platform does, per Monaco
- **Build the market:** automatically build and score a startup's total addressable market (TAM), producing a stack-ranked list of target accounts.
- **Find buyers:** identify recommended buyers, incorporating signals such as existing connections, job changes, and custom web-based signals.
- **Run outbound:** generate custom outreach campaigns using embedded go-to-market best practices.
- **Capture interactions:** automate sales call note-taking through a meeting recorder, generate email drafts, and update the system of record (creating and tracking opportunities, updating form fields).
- **Coach closing:** give advice on how to close more deals, while remaining customizable to each company's sales motion.
- **Human-in-the-loop:** Monaco's stated approach combines AI-native automation with experienced human salespeople as experts in the loop — monitoring and guiding the AI's work, with actual customer meetings conducted by human representatives, not avatars, per launch coverage.
