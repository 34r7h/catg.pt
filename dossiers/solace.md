## 1. What it is
**Solace Health is a digital platform that connects patients with dedicated healthcare advocates who navigate the healthcare system on their behalf.**
- **Advocates:** registered nurses and other healthcare professionals with an average of 16 years of clinical experience, per Solace's own descriptions. Each patient works with the same dedicated advocate throughout their journey, by phone, video, text, or email.
- **What advocates do:** coordinate appointments, manage treatment plans, coordinate between doctors, manage medical records, appeal denied claims, and ensure continuity of care.
- **Founded 2022 by Jeremy Gurewitz and Sara Sargent** after their own experiences with a fragmented healthcare system, per Solace descriptions.
- **Coverage, per Solace:** covered nationwide by Medicare and most major commercial insurance plans, including UnitedHealthcare and Anthem Blue Cross Blue Shield, with eligibility varying by plan. Advocates do not provide medical or legal advice or services, per Solace's own terms.

## 2. How it is used
- Check eligibility (described as taking less than a minute), schedule a short introductory call to understand healthcare challenges, then meet a dedicated advocate and make a plan.
- Advocates spend additional hours behind the scenes coordinating care for every hour spent directly with a patient, per Solace descriptions.
