# NYC Stoop — distilled knowledge (from the App Store listing as of 2026-10-19; developer site unreachable)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**NYC Stoop is a curated iOS guide to New York — "a few hundred places worth your time, chosen and described like a friend who lives there would, not a database of everything with a star rating."** It's an editorial field guide, not a reviews aggregator. Free to download and explore, with **no ads — ever**. Supports private photo sharing with friends.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Traveler: "Tell us when you're starting, your budget, and where — one tap builds a routed outing that actually fits the budget you picked."
- Local sharing with friends: "Share photos from the places you love (up to 5 per post, each with caption and place pin) — visible to friends only, never public."
- Planner: "Your saves turn into a routed day: grouped by neighborhood, with walking times and transit directions."

## Features (App Store description)
- **Explore:** browse by what you feel like doing — eat, drink, see art, hear live music, walk a neighborhood. Every place has an editorial description and an insider tip (what to order, when to go, which entrance to use).
- **Eat & Drink:** restaurants, cafés, bars across NYC, sorted by what's near you or by recommendations when planning from home.
- **Events:** live listings (concerts, shows, games) you can pin straight into your trip days.
- **Trips:** tap + on anything → saves turn into a routed day: grouped by neighborhood, with walking times and transit directions (which train/bus to take, where to board/off, how far to walk).
- **Plan It!:** "Tell us when you're starting, your budget, and where — one tap builds a routed outing that actually fits the budget you picked."
- **Your Stoop (new in 2.0):** share photos (up to 5 per post, each with its own caption and place pin) — every shared photo lands on your personal map. **Connect with private friend codes**: your photos are visible to friends only, never public. Comment on each other's finds, or add a friend's spot straight to your plan. The built-in **@NYC Stoop** account is your first friend, with curated spots across the city.
- Extras: neighborhood maps, weather-aware suggestions, comments and photo sharing that strip location metadata for privacy, "a design that feels like a beautiful field guide, not an app full of ads."
