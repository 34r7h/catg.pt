## 1. What it is
**Wonderful is an enterprise AI company that provides an AI operating system — a shared operating layer for deploying, coordinating, and governing AI agents across a large enterprise.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — Israel #3, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Official site:** wonderful.ai. Not wonderfulagent.ai, a separate self-serve agent product site — do not confuse the two.
- **Founded in 2025 by Bar Winkler (CEO) and Roey Lalazar (CTO),** per the company's own releases, with main offices in Tel Aviv and Amsterdam.

## 2. What it does
- **The AI OS:** a shared layer that coordinates agents, workflows, AI-native applications, enterprise context, and integrations, then governs how all of it executes across the organisation, fitted to the systems each customer already runs, per Wonderful's own description. It is model-agnostic and application-universal, rather than betting on a single foundation model or a single vertical use case.
- **Where agents run:** customer service, IT, and other core operations, per LinkedIn's list entry, across front and back office — telcos, financial services, healthcare, and manufacturing are named as deployment sectors in company releases.
- **How it deploys:** forward-deployed engineering pods co-build alongside customer teams in every market, getting AI into production and transferring capability so enterprises can increasingly build on the platform themselves, per Wonderful's own description. The platform is deployable across cloud environments including on-premise, per company releases.
- **Claimed impact, per company releases:** handling-time reductions of up to 60% and containment rates above 80% across deployments. Treat these as Wonderful claims, not independently verified figures.
