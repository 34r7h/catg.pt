## 1. What it is
**Emergent is an agentic vibe-coding platform that turns a plain-language description into a full-stack, production-ready application.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #7, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Multi-agent builds:** instead of one model doing everything, specialised agents plan, build, test, and deploy the software, and attempt to diagnose and fix errors before handing control back.
- **What a build includes:** a real database, a login system, and third-party integrations, with an AI agent as one feature of the app — not just a generated front end.
- **AI Agent Builder:** describe an agent in chat, review the build, test it, and deploy it — as a standalone app on your own domain or embedded as a copilot in an existing product. Agents reason, use tools, and act within boundaries the builder sets, including which steps need a person's approval first, and every action is recorded in an audit trail.
- **Disambiguation:** Emergent (emergent.sh) is not emergentai.pro, a separate site with a similar name. Do not mix the two products.

## 2. How it is used
- A founder or team describes an app idea in plain language, previews the result, and refines it through the same conversation.
- A team builds an internal tool, dashboard, or customer-facing app without an engineering team, then owns the generated code and database and can host, modify, or export it.
- An agent is scoped carefully up front — task, tools, tone, and one thing it must never do — and tested against awkward cases before anything customer-facing goes live.
