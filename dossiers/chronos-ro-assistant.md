# Chronos — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Chronos (chronos.ro, by Chronos Labs) is an AI-native GTM (go-to-market) platform — "Your AI GTM Team" — that automates outbound sales for founders and sales teams.** You paste your website; Chronos builds a "GTM digital twin" of your company, then sources leads from a 350M-lead database, writes personalized messages in your voice and runs multi-channel campaigns (LinkedIn, email, SMS, X, Instagram, Facebook, Reddit, Substack, AgentMail) — with pipeline and deal intelligence on top. Founded 2024, HQ San Francisco; the site claims it is VC backed and "Backed by Sam Altman".

**One line for different audiences:**
- Founder: "Paste your website and every outbound channel runs on autopilot, in your voice."
- Sales team / SDR lead: "Outbound intelligence + pipeline mastery + deal coaching you command in plain English; plug in any AI agent via MCP."
- Enterprise: "Managed GTM deployment with SSO, advanced controls and security/procurement review."

## How it works (4 steps, per /info)
1. **Paste your website** — no setup calls; Chronos reads your site, learns product, tone, ICP and differentiators.
2. **It builds your GTM digital twin** — messaging, audience targeting and channel strategy configured within minutes, calibrated to sound like you.
3. **Channels get spun up** — fresh warmed email domains provisioned; LinkedIn, X, AgentMail, SMS, Instagram, Facebook run in parallel from one paste.
4. **Campaigns run themselves** — identifies ideal customers from the 350M-lead database, writes personalized messages, sends recommended daily volume per channel, paced for deliverability.
