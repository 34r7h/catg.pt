## 1. What it is
**Cadence is a health technology company that helps large health systems deliver remote care outside the hospital — remote patient monitoring (RPM) and preventive primary care for chronic conditions.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #4.
- **Domain note:** the live official domain is cadence.care, not cadencehealth.com.
- **Care in Sync platform:** couples technology with clinical excellence, pairing patients with a Cadence care team that acts as an extension of the ordering physician.
- **Conditions covered:** congestive heart failure, type 2 diabetes, hypertension, and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).
- **How RPM works:** a physician refers a patient; a Cadence patient educator enrolls them and provides cellular-enabled devices (blood pressure monitor, weight scale); the patient takes daily readings at home, transmitted wirelessly to Cadence clinicians and the ordering physician.
- **Clinical Intelligence:** AI that automates routine care and protocol-driven decisions, per the company's site — including physician-led medication titration under nationally recognized, evidence-based guidelines — while keeping physicians in control; the company reports 99% of alerts resolved without physician escalation, a company figure, not an independently verified outcome.
- **Preventive primary care:** a Medicare-covered model that adds 24/7 symptom checks, care-gap closure, and lifestyle coaching alongside RPM.

## 2. How it is used
- A patient takes daily vitals at home; Cadence clinicians review readings, reach out by text or phone when a reading is high, and coordinate medication changes with the ordering provider.
- Health systems use Cadence to extend chronic care beyond clinic visits, including post-acute support after hospital discharge to avoid readmission.
