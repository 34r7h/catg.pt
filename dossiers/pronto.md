## 1. What it is
**Pronto is an on-demand home-services platform in India that sends verified, uniformed professionals to a customer's home in minutes.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #12, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Official site:** withpronto.com.
- **Core promise, per its own site:** trusted house help in minutes, booked through the Pronto app, with support via help@withpronto.com or in-app chat.
- **Founder, per public coverage:** Anjali Sardana.

## 2. What it does
- **Services:** cleaning (sweeping, mopping, dusting, kitchen and bathroom cleaning), utensil washing and dishwashing, laundry, and basic meal preparation such as vegetable chopping, per public coverage and the official site's service framing.
- **Booking modes:** instant, scheduled ahead, and recurring, per public coverage; the official site lists live availability in 11 Indian cities, including Bangalore, Delhi, Faridabad, Ghaziabad, Gurgaon, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Noida, Pune, and Thane.
- **Pricing, per the official site:** transparent and shown upfront in the app, priced per visit with hourly bundles starting from 30-minute slots and per-task prices for services such as bathroom cleaning or utensil washing — no per-bedroom multipliers, no hidden charges, no advance deposits, per withpronto.com. Live prices vary by locality; check the app for the address in question.
- **How it differs from an informal hire:** professionals are background-verified, trained, and uniformed, and bookings carry live tracking, per public coverage.
