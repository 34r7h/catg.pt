## 1. What it is
**Scapia is a travel-fintech company whose core product is a co-branded travel credit card, issued with Federal Bank, wrapped in a travel-booking app.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #19, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.

## 2. What it does
- **The card:** a Federal Bank co-branded credit card, accepted in India and over 150 countries, per Scapia's own releases, with fully digital onboarding in the Scapia app and instant virtual-card activation on approval, per public guides.
- **Card terms, per Scapia's own releases:** zero joining fee, zero annual fee, and zero forex markup on international spends, with unlimited domestic lounge access subject to the applicable monthly spend condition. Reward and benefit details vary by card variant — check the terms attached to the specific variant before relying on any figure.
- **Rewards, per Scapia's own releases:** rewards issued as Scapia Coins, redeemable in the app for flights, hotels, buses, trains, and visas, at 5 Scapia Coins = ₹1. Headline reward rates in Scapia's own material reach 20% on travel bookings made through the Scapia app and 10% on eligible everyday spending — those percentages are coin-earning rates, not equivalent cash-back percentages.
- **The travel platform in the app:** flight, hotel, bus, train, and visa bookings, with features including custom flight add-ons, Scapia Assist travel assistance, "Travel Now, Pay Later," and 24/7 customer service, per Scapia's own releases.
