## 1. What it is
**Rillet is an AI-native accounting platform — an agentic ERP — built for modern finance teams.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #22.
- **Positioning, per company releases:** the Agentic ERP, combining a real-time general ledger, continuous close architecture, and AI agents in one system, so humans and agents run finance together with full context, controls, and auditability.
- **Founded in 2021,** per public profiles, aimed at corporate finance teams as an alternative to traditional ERP systems.

## 2. How it works
- **Real-time general ledger:** per company releases, the ledger is continuously updated, so teams operate from an always-current view of the business instead of a periodic close.
- **Continuous / zero-day close:** per company releases, Rillet is built for the zero-day close — finance work closes continuously rather than in a month-end crunch.
- **AI agents in accounting workflows:** per company releases, agents are embedded directly in workflows, with every entry carrying its reasoning back to the source document, and a complete audit trail of every agent action.
- **Audit and pay:** per rillet.com product pages, the platform ingests every invoice in every format (EDI, PDF, email, API), validates every line item against contracted rates and service terms, and resolves exceptions automatically, including notifying carriers and closing disputes.
