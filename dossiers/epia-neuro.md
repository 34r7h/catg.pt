## 1. What it is
**EPIA NEURO is a neurotechnology company building products that learn the brain's intent and carry it out in the physical world, giving people back independence.**
- **First focus: stroke.** Its first product is built to restore hand function after stroke, where the pathway from intent to movement is damaged.
- **How the system works, per the company:** a thought drives intent, a world model perceives the scene, a policy selects the movement, and a controller carries it out through a grip-assist exo.
- **Brain interface:** a proprietary implant that learns intent from the brain and how each person expresses it, without penetrating the brain or the membrane that protects it, per epianeuro.com.
- **Grip-assist device:** a motorised glove / upper-limb grip-assist prosthetic that translates decoded intent into physical hand movement, described as simple and replaceable for daily use.

## 2. How it is used
- For stroke survivors who retain neural intent to move but have lost the ability to translate that intent into action.
- First-in-human system demonstrations were planned for 2026 at Lenox Hill Hospital's Department of Neurosurgery in New York, per April 2026 launch reporting. Do not present this as a commercially available medical product — it is an early-stage system in development, with no FDA approval stated in this context.
