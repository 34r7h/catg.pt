# Audacious AF — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Audacious AF (branded "Audacious AI Friends") is an in-person New York City community for women leaders who are building, or starting to build, with AI — hands-on workshops, salon-style discussions, skill shares and meetups in intentionally small rooms.** Founded in 2026 by Maggie Blackburn, Paige Kehoe and Samantha Provenza. Events run on Luma (luma.com/audaciousaf); the community site is audaciousaf.com. Its stated mission: "Closing the AI gender gap one skillshare, workshop and meetup at a time."

**One line per audience:**
- Member/attendee: "Small, hands-on NYC rooms where you can talk about what excites, scares and overwhelms you about AI — without explaining your background first."
- Partner/sponsor: "The women in our rooms are changing how their companies work with AI. Get in the room with them — no sales pitches."
- Host: "Open your office or studio for a workshop — Wi-Fi, a screen, and room for laptops is all we need."

## How it works
1. **Discover** — events listed on audaciousaf.com/events and luma.com/audaciousaf.
2. **Register on Luma** — free RSVP, paid ticket, or approval-required ("Request to Join"); some locations hidden until you register.
3. **Show up IRL** — mostly in person in NYC, some virtual; workshops are laptop-open, "you leave having made something."
4. **Membership (coming)** — join the waitlist at audaciousaf.com/join; applications open → founding cohort admitted. "Founding membership is coming" — no price published.
5. **Partner** — host a night, sponsor, put a product in the room, send a speaker, offer a member perk, or run a series (three sponsorship levels).

## Positioning / objections (site's own words)
- **"Is it a big conference / online course?"** No — "We keep rooms intentionally small and prioritize depth over volume. By invitation or application only." Membership: "Mostly in person, NYC · Some virtual."
- **"Is it beginner-friendly?"** Yes — "whether you've already started playing around with vibe coding or just getting started, all are welcome"; workshops are "for business owners, not engineers… No technical background needed."
- **"Is membership open now?"** Not yet — "Founding membership is coming. Get on the waitlist." Steps: waitlist → applications open → founding cohort admitted.
- **"Can men attend?"** Individual events: "open to any women identifying individual who wants to learn how to do more with AI" (the Claude SMB virtual event says it "is open to all individuals"). Membership targets women leaders.
- **"Is it a sales pitch for sponsors?"** "Nobody is here for a sales pitch, so we don't do those." Sponsors keep meetups free.
- **Claude/Anthropic relationship:** Audacious AF is a member of the **Claude Partner Network**; Claude workshops are delivered by **Claude Certified Trainers** with proprietary materials.
