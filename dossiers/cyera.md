## 1. What it is
**Cyera is a data security company — a pioneer in data security posture management (DSPM).** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #30 (it also appears at #6 on LinkedIn's Israel 2026 list, reflecting its Tel Aviv R&D center).
- **Founded in 2021, headquartered in New York City, with a research and development center in Tel Aviv,** per public profiles; founders Yotam Segev, Tamar Bar-Ilan, and Yonatan Itay.
- **Purpose, per the company:** give businesses deep context on their data, applying proper, continuous controls to assure cyber-resilience and compliance.

## 2. How it works
- **Discover:** Cyera automatically and continuously scans data stores to learn what data exists, where it is located, who can access it, and whether it is at risk, across cloud, SaaS, and — per company releases — on-premises databases and file shares.
- **Assess risk in context:** per the company, visibility over sensitive data plus context over the risk it represents and the security exposure, so teams prioritize remediation by material risk rather than raw alert volume.
- **Remediate:** per the company, automated remediation reduces the attack surface, and Data Detection and Response (DDR) plus a Unified Data Explorer cover SaaS, IaaS, and PaaS, per a 2026 company release.
- **AI governance:** per company materials, Cyera's AI security products discover AI tools in use (including shadow AI), inventory them by owner and data store, and monitor agent actions, prompts, and responses for sensitive-data leakage and misuse.
- **Domain note:** older company materials point to cyera.io; current company pages and profiles use cyera.com, which is the domain asserted here.
