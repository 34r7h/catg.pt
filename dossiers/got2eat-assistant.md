# Got2Eat — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Got2Eat is a free consumer food app: a community-verified map of filling meals under $15, hand-picked by locals.** You see the price on the map before you walk in — "no menu-hunting, no surprises at the till." The site states its principles in the header: **"No ads · No selling data."**

**One line for different audiences:**
- Diner: "Filling meals under $15 near you, every price on the map before you go — free."
- Restaurant: "List your spot / promos to local users who come to eat — get in touch via the site."

## How it works (as far as the site shows)
1. Open got2eat.app → the map shows spots near you ("Finding spots near you…", "Loading restaurants…").
2. Every listed spot shows its price ("Every price on the map before you walk. No menu-hunting, no surprises at the till.").
3. Sign in (Continue with Google / Apple / email) → "Your savings receipt, your saved spots and every price you've checked — on whatever phone you're holding."
4. The non-JS fallback of the site (and got2eat.site) describes the product as: "Got2eat helps people discover budget-friendly meals, city lunch deals, partner promos, and local spots with notes and photos."
5. The static demo on got2eat.site shows the deal format: dish name, venue, walk time, price (e.g. "Fried Dumplings (4 pieces) · Jubilee Marketplace · 6 min · $2"); app tabs: Nearby, Deals, Activity, About, Profile.

## Per audience

### Diners (primary)
- Value proposition: eat well without overspending; hand-picked filling meals under $15; prices upfront on a map.
- Account: sign-in with Google/Apple/email; your savings receipt, saved spots and checked prices follow you across devices.
- Focus markets per the founder's own site (jaisonj.xyz, 2026-10-19): NYC & Jersey City; the site however has city pages for 11 US metros.
- Founder-hosted "meal-up" community events exist (event listings, e.g. via Luma/Pie, first listed Aug 2026; described as "the $15 lunch that eats like $30") — per event pages, not the app itself.

### Restaurants / local spots
- The site's only partner CTA: "Are you a restaurant? Get in touch with us!" — no partner portal, terms or pricing shown on the site.

## Positioning / objections
- **vs delivery apps:** Got2Eat shows you where to go and what it costs; it is not a delivery or ordering service (nothing on the site offers delivery/ordering).
- **vs review apps:** the differentiator the site stresses is price transparency ("Every price on the map before you walk… no surprises at the till") plus the "No ads · No selling data" promise.
- **Caution:** a similarly named Google Play app "Got 2 Eat" (developer Go Host Ltd, UK — takeaway search and shared group orders) is a different product. Do not attribute its features to got2eat.app.

## Specifics for answers
- The site is a JavaScript app; most subpage content is not readable outside the browser. If a question is about a specific city's listings — say the map in the browser is the source of truth; do not invent venues or prices.
- Founder context (only when asked who's behind it; attribute to founder's own site/LinkedIn, not the app): Jaison Jayaraj, NYC; Base44 Accelerator top-2 finish / Demo Day finalist (Cohort 1); building full-time since Jul 2026; per his site, raising $750K pre-seed.
- No pricing tiers, no ads, no data selling — the site is free for users and states it doesn't sell data.
