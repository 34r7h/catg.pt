# Streamex.com — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-18)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Streamex builds infrastructure that transforms commodities into programmable financial products.** It aims to make commodities ownable, tradable, programmable, yield-bearing, collateralizable, and globally accessible via a unified financial layer. Its first product is **GLDY** (tokenized gold, ~3.5% targeted yield).

**One line for different audiences:**
- Institutional investor: "Infrastructure for tokenized commodities — programmable tokens with yield, lending/collateral use cases."
- Commodity participant: "Transforms physical commodities into programmable financial products with unified settlement/access."
- Partner: "Platform to build financial products on top of tokenized commodities."

## How it works (from site)
- **Tokenize** — Commodities (gold, silver, copper, crude oil, more to come) become programmable tokens.
- **Unify** — Single financial layer replacing siloed custodians/brokers/regulations/settlement/liquidity.
- **Enable** — Unlocks yield, lending, collateral use, integration into financial products, global distribution, instant liquidity, transparent reporting.
