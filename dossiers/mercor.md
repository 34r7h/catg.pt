## 1. What it is
**Mercor is an AI data company building the layer between human expertise and frontier AI models.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #2.
- **Mission, in Mercor's own words:** organizing human intelligence to power the AI economy.
- **Founded in 2023, headquartered in San Francisco,** per public company profiles.
- **What it does:** partners with labs and enterprises building advanced AI systems; a global network of domain experts trains frontier models, runs evaluations, and produces labeled data.

## 2. How it works
- **Work:** experts join a global network doing high-value work at the frontier of AI.
- **Build:** partner for custom AI agents trained on an organization's knowledge, workflows, and standards.
- **Hire:** access domain experts across 300+ professional fields, found and vetted using dynamic, role-specific AI interviews, per mercor.com.
- **Evaluate:** measure AI model effectiveness at economically valuable work with Mercor's APEX benchmark family.
- **Enterprise:** Mercor Enterprise brings the same expert network and evaluation infrastructure to Fortune 500 companies deploying AI in production, per mercor.com.
