## 1. What it is
**Basic Capital is a fintech company redesigning long-term investing around financing for productive assets.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #20.
- **The core idea, per basiccapital.com:** access to credit to buy productive assets has been the foundation of wealth building in America for cars, education, and homes; Basic Capital applies the same idea to a long-term investment portfolio.
- **Mortgage analogy, per basiccapital.com:** a mortgage lets a buyer own a $500,000 home with $100,000 down; Basic Capital aims to do the same for a retirement portfolio — start with more investing power from day one, so compounding works on a larger base for longer.
- **Retirement plans, per third-party profiles:** Basic Capital also provides 401(k) plans and software for employers and advisors, including recordkeeping, plan administration, compliance monitoring, and a brokerage window.

## 2. How it works
- **For every dollar contributed, Basic Capital invests alongside the contributor,** per basiccapital.com; returns cover the cost of financing, and the spread is kept by the contributor.
- **Built-in protection, per basiccapital.com:** money is invested through a Limited Liability Company wholly owned by the contributor, so exposure is limited to what is contributed; the financing is long-term, with no margin calls and no minimum equity requirements, per the company.
- **Asset mix:** build from stocks, bonds, or a broader mix of eligible assets, per basiccapital.com.
- **Why lenders participate, per basiccapital.com:** financing providers seek predictable cash flows, while clients seek long-term investment risk and reward — the same split as a bank earning interest on a mortgage while the homeowner takes the appreciation.
