# Treetracker — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-18)

## What it is (30 seconds)
Treetracker is a climate/wildfire prevention startup building low-cost ground sensors that turn the earliest environmental signals into localized wildfire alerts. It aims to fill the gap where satellites/aircraft can be delayed or not localized enough — delivering earlier ground-level warnings to communities/land managers.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Land managers/utilities: "Low-cost ground sensors + localized alerts to detect wildfire risk earlier at the hotspot level."
- Technical cofounder candidates: "Hardware/embedded systems startup building field-ready wildfire detection sensors (pre-deployment, device in active build)."

## How it works (4 layers)
1. **Sense the ground** — Distributed field devices collect temperature, particulate, humidity, wind data in hotspot zones.
2. **Read the signal** — Edge intelligence processes signals.
3. **Move the data** — Long-range communication moves data.
4. **Warn locally** — Localized alerts delivered.
