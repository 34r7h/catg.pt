# LotusPetalAI — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
LotusPetal AI (lotuspetal.ai) sells **PetalOS™**, "AI RFP Software for GovCon & Commercial Teams" — a pursuit-intelligence platform whose pitch is the five-word tagline **"Find. Qualify. Capture. Propose. Win."** It unifies opportunity discovery, capture management, compliance tracking, and AI proposal generation in one system built for structured procurement environments. Security is a first-class selling point: SOC-2 Type II Certified, FedRAMP High Aligned, Perfect VAPT Score — shown as badges in the hero.

**By audience:**
- *GovCon contractors:* platform to discover high-fit opportunities the moment they emerge and produce evaluator-ready proposals faster.
- *Commercial RFP teams:* the same end-to-end capture-to-proposal workflow for non-government RFPs (site explicitly says it is "not only for government contracting").
- *Capture managers:* capture plan backed by competitive intel "before you write a word."
- *Proposal writers:* AI drafting from proven win intelligence and your own knowledge, with early requirement tracking.

## How it works
**PetalOS™ — "One Engine, Every Stage"**: one intelligence layer across the entire pursuit; the cycle repeats and gets smarter every time. Five stages:
1. **Discover** — "Opportunities surfaced the moment they emerge."
2. **Match Fit** — "Matched to your capabilities and past performance."
3. **Capture** — "The capture plan, backed by real competitive intel, before you write a word."
4. **Compliance** — "Every requirement surfaced early, tracked and verified as you draft."
5. **Propose** — "Evaluator-ready proposals, built from proven win intelligence and your knowledge."

Two solution pillars: **Capture Management** ("Skip the guesswork. Start where you're strongest." — sample: DoD "Predictive Maintenance Platform – RFP #A12345, Posted 8h ago, Strong Match") and **Proposal Generation** ("Generate winning proposals from scratch, powered by AI").

Assistant prompts shown on the page: "Suggest a win theme", "Draft a mission-aligned statement", "Write our approach to risk mitigation", "Create a compliant staffing plan", "Tell me the milestones", "Who's the SME for the cybersecurity section?", "Summarize the key requirements in Section C."

## Positioning / objections
- **"Is it just a chatbot on top of an RFP?"** No: the differentiator (per founder's external interviews) is *vertical* models trained for capture management/procurement, versus uploading sensitive data into a general-purpose LLM. "Containerized Proposal Intelligence Core" per LinkedIn.
- **"Is our data safe?"** "Your Data, Your Control": no training on your data, no reuse, no sharing; AES-256 at rest and in transit; FedRAMP High Aligned, SOC 2 Type II, "aligned to FISMA and ITAR requirements." External interviews add: hosted on AWS GovCloud, containerized data to prevent cross-IP contamination.
- **"Government only?"** No — five industries: Government, Consulting, Manufacturing, Construction, Healthcare.
- **Buyer journey:** everything is demo-gated ("Request Demo" / "Find My Opportunity" → /demo/ form with name, phone, email, job title, company, company size). No self-serve signup or public pricing.
