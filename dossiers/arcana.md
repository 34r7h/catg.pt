Source: LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #48; arcana.io product descriptions as summarized in public coverage, accessed 2026-10-29. Pages skipped: none in this distillation — this is a listing-plus-public-product distillation, not a full site crawl; full site capture is pending.

## 1. What it is
**Arcana is a portfolio intelligence platform built for hedge funds and asset managers.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #48, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **One connected view:** portfolio construction, performance attribution, risk management, research, alpha capture, and AI-powered workflows in a single institutional-grade platform.
- **What teams ask it:** what is driving portfolio risk and performance, how exposures have changed, where crowding sits, how positioning compares with a benchmark, and what explains single-stock and book-level performance.
- **Data foundation:** per public coverage, proprietary crowding, ownership, factor risk, and performance datasets support scenario analysis, optimization, and idea screening.
- **Where it surfaces:** web, desktop, and iOS apps, plus Excel, APIs, automated reporting, and AI interfaces including Claude — so investors can ask questions in natural language grounded in their own portfolio data.

## 2. How it is used
- An investment team monitors intraday performance and attribution — positions, factor exposures, contributors — and drills into individual winners and losers.
- Analysts screen ideas and test scenarios against the book, rather than assembling risk and performance views from separate tools.
- Teams review research notes and proprietary data (price targets, conviction scores) alongside the portfolio, keeping investment context connected to positions.
