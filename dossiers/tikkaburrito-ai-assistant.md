## What it is (30 seconds)
TikkaBurrito is a no-code AI website/app builder that turns typed or spoken ideas into working web apps/websites. The distinctive feature is "phone-call vibe coding": describe what you need by phone call (or text in browser), AI designs/builds/tests/launches, and delivers the live website/app link via SMS or WhatsApp.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Non-technical users: "Build a business website/app with a phone call — no coding, no complex prompts. Get a live link by SMS/WhatsApp."
- Builders/startups: "AI builder that generates real React code, lets you iterate, preview, and deploy to a live URL."

1. **Call & talk** — Tell TikkaBurrito what you need in your own words (phone call) or via browser.
2. **AI builds it** — AI designs, builds, tests, and launches; generates real app files.
