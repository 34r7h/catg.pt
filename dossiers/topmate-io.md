Source: LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #2; topmate.io product pages as summarized in public coverage, accessed 2026-10-29. Pages skipped: none in this distillation — this is a listing-plus-public-product distillation, not a full site crawl; full site capture is pending.

## 1. What it is
**Topmate is a platform where experts sell 1:1 consultations, digital products, and memberships from a single page.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #2, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **For experts:** one page for calls, webinars, courses, digital products, packages, priority DMs, and memberships, with Topmate taking a commission on sales.
- **For buyers:** search by what you need, then book a call or buy a product on the expert's page — no page of your own is needed to book.
- **Typical services:** career advice, mock interviews, resume and LinkedIn-profile reviews, job referrals, salary negotiation, startup guidance, and technical interview preparation, plus many other fields from marketing to wellbeing.
- **Calls:** 1:1 video or audio consultations run on Topmate's call rooms, or via connected Google Meet or the expert's own meeting link.

## 2. How it is used
- An expert sets up a page, lists services with prices, and shares the link with their audience; bookings and payments run through Topmate.
- A buyer who already knows whose time they want books directly; a buyer without a name in mind searches by topic instead.
- Refund handling follows Topmate's published policy — for example, expert no-show or short-notice cancellation generally qualifies, while subjective dissatisfaction with advice that was delivered generally does not.
