# YESODI — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-18)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**YESODI** is a social commerce platform that positions itself as "where your business sells itself." The site states: "Build your professional identity on Yesodi. Bring together who you are, your companies, brands, products, services, articles, videos, music and professional work in one connected profile that is found on Google and AI search." Its tagline is **"Publish. Sell. Connect."** The platform aims to help monetize every post, product, search, and link via a unified professional identity.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Creators/founders: "One connected professional identity that brings together your work, brands, and products — publish, sell, connect."
- Businesses: "Monetize every post, product, search and link; your business sells itself through one discoverable profile."
- Community organizers: "A hub with events, channels, marketplace, and directories (people/brands/business) with content feeds (posts, intel, videos, music, news)."

## How it works / features (from homepage)
1. **Professional identity/profile** — Single connected profile combining companies, brands, products, services, articles, videos, music, professional work.
2. **Monetization** — "Monetize every post, product, search and link."
3. **Discoverability** — Profiles and content are "found on Google and AI search."
4. **Content feeds** — Latest Posts, Intel Articles, Community Videos, Community Music, Community News; posts with titles, snippets, links to individual items.
5. **Directories & marketplace** — People Directory (/people), Brands Directory (/brands), Business Directory (/directory), Marketplace (/explore), Events (/events).
6. **Channels** — Thematic channels (e.g., Personal Development, Travel, Aviation, Tennis, Movies, Real Estate, Business & Deals, plus community channels).
7. **Community events** — Calendar of events listed (many upcoming/past listed on homepage).
