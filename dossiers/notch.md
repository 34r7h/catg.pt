## 1. What it is
**Notch is an AI platform that deploys policy-governed AI agents to autonomously resolve complex customer support operations, built for regulated industries.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — Israel #7.
- **Full name:** Notch CX; the company is referred to here as Notch, the name on the LinkedIn list.
- **Where it operates:** insurance, eCommerce, SaaS, and gaming, with a strong insurance focus — first notice of loss (FNOL) triage, claims communications, endorsements, coverage verifications, and policy servicing.
- **How it works:** agents connect to existing systems of record (policy admin, claims management, payment systems) and execute policies autonomously, rather than only drafting replies; state-specific compliance rules and timelines are built into the workflows.
- **ADAM:** Notch's product for understanding an entire operation — it evaluates the processes behind interactions, flags outdated procedures, policy gaps, and compliance blind spots, and recommends workflow or agent changes.

## 2. How it is used
- A policyholder contacts support and a Notch agent resolves the request end to end — for example triaging FNOL, requesting and validating documentation, or handling an endorsement — instead of routing it to a human queue.
- Teams use ADAM to find why interactions escalate, then build or fix agents and policies around those bottlenecks.
- Resolution is defined strictly in Notch's own model: full end-to-end autonomous handling, not deflection or containment.
