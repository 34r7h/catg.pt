## 1. What it is
**Dandy is a fully digital dental lab building the modern operating system for the North American dental office — intraoral scanning, expert guidance, and digital lab manufacturing in one integrated system.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #44.
- **100% digital, no physical impressions:** Dandy does not accept physical impressions; every case runs digitally, which the company says delivers greater precision and industry-leading turnaround times.
- **What's included:** a state-of-the-art intraoral scanner (the Dandy Vision scanner, free with a monthly lab minimum), always-on expert guidance, and AI-powered Chairside software, all orchestrated through Dandy's proprietary portal.
- **Full-service product range:** crown and bridge, implants, full dentures, partial dentures, splints and guards, sleep apnea devices, surgical guides, and clear aligners.
- **Manufacturing:** fixed restorations produced in Dandy's lab in Lehi, Utah, and removable products in Carrollton, Texas, using robots, machine learning, and AI, with generative AI and 3D printing in the workflow; clear aligners are manufactured in Germany through a partner.
- **Workflow examples, per the company:** 5-day zirconia crowns, 2-appointment dentures, straight-to-finish partials, and a 3-appointment smile makeover for anterior cosmetic dentistry.
- **Support model:** live scan review with a lab technician (scans reviewed in under two minutes while the patient is still in the chair), design previews so dentists approve crown designs before production, and free remakes.

## 2. How it is used
- A dentist scans a patient with the Dandy scanner, gets live help perfecting the scan, approves the digital design, and tracks the case to delivery in the portal — month-to-month, with no long-term contract, per the company's site.
- Practices use it to replace fragmented analog lab workflows, remake-prone cases, and constant design revisions.
