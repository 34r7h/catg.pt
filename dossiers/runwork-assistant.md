# Runwork — distilled knowledge (from runwork.ai, crawled 2026-10-18; reviewed 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Runwork is a team cloud that sits underneath existing AI tools** (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, etc.). It gives teams shared "spaces" (per piece of work) containing files, AI instructions, chats, skills, apps/automations, data/integrations, permissions and audit. Teams keep using the tools they already prefer; Runwork preserves and shares what they learn/build.

**One line per audience:**
- Founder/Head of AI: "Make AI work compound — what anyone learns/builds with AI stays shared and reusable across tools."
- Team member: "Start chats in any AI tool with the same space context (files, skills, instructions)."
- Eng/ops/admin: "Deploy AI-built apps/automations in spaces with permissions, audit, and 3,600+ integrations/MCP."

## How it works (core ideas)
1. **Spaces** — One space per kind of work (e.g. onboarding, renewals). Holds files, AI instructions, chats, skills, apps/automations, data/integrations.
2. **Context follows you** — Start conversations from space in your preferred tool (Claude/ChatGPT/Gemini/Copilot etc.). Runwork surfaces space context; chats stay on local machine unless shared.
3. **Share selectively** — Files/chats stay private until shared; changes tracked so team can pick what to share.
4. **Skills** — Save "how to do X" once (prompt/workflow) as a skill; everyone’s AI can use it.
5. **Apps & automations** — Describe what you need; AI builds app/automation in space; Runwork hosts it with auth/data/integrations.
6. **Integrations** — Connect accounts (HubSpot, Slack, Google Drive, Notion, Jira, GitHub, etc., 3,600+); credentials managed via Runwork; every call traceable.

## Use cases
- **Team onboarding to AI**: Give new people everything (files, instructions, skills) from day one.
- **Preserve best practices**: Turn proven prompts/workflows into reusable skills.
- **Automate workflows**: Build internal tools/automations from natural language; run in space.
- **Cross-tool collaboration**: Different people use different tools but share same space context.
