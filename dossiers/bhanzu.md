## 1. What it is
**Bhanzu is a math-education platform for children that teaches mathematics through personalised, live, AI-aided lessons designed to replace math fear with confidence.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #18, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Official site:** bhanzu.com (bhanzu.us is used for its US presence).
- **Founded in November 2020 by Neelakantha Bhanu Prakash,** the "World's Fastest Human Calculator" — first Asian to win gold at the Mind Sports Olympiad, per company releases.

## 2. How it is used
- **Live, personalised lessons:** students learn in live sessions with real-time feedback, regular progress reports, and a roadmap for parents, rather than passive screen time, per company descriptions.
- **Curriculum:** a research-backed curriculum rooted in cognitive science and educational psychology, covering foundations through competitive preparation — Bhanzu's own pages list AMC 8, AMC 10, Math Kangaroo, and MATHCOUNTS for its competitive track, alongside fractions, mental math, and logical reasoning.
- **Method:** strong concepts and logic first, not formula memorisation, so students can handle unfamiliar problems as well as familiar ones, per Bhanzu's own framing; a hybrid of online and in-person classes is offered in some locations.
- **Positioning:** math as confidence-building and real-world application, including STEM readiness, rather than test preparation alone.
