## What it is (30 seconds)
**MedStartr.ai is a healthcare innovation marketplace and community** — "Connect, Collaborate and Create the Future of Care." It lists healthcare companies, connects them with the crowd (patients, care pros, adopters, investors, mentors), and runs events, contests and matchmaking. ## How it works
1. **Find** — filter companies by clinical area, category, technology, funding/product stage, country, state, city, total raised, founded; "My Matches", Leaderboards, Advanced search.
2. **Engage** — every company has reaction/lead buttons: 👍 Like · ❤️ Love · 🔥 Fire · 💬 Message · 🛒 Try/Buy · 🧠 Mentor · 👥 Join · ✈️ Pilot · 🤝 Partner · 💰 Invest (actions that lead somewhere require sign-in).
3. **Community** — mentors (Meet/Become a Mentor/Apply), communities By City (By Topic coming soon), events (All Upcoming/Past), contests (Challenges), shop (/shop, Selling on MedStartr).
4. **Account** — Join free (/sign_up); deeper data is member-gated ("Only accessible to MedStartr community members").
