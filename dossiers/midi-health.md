## 1. What it is

## 2. How it works
- **Book online, meet by video:** per public guides, confirm insurance, book online, meet a clinician by secure video to review history, symptoms, and goals, then start a personalized plan.
- **Plans may include** hormonal or non-hormonal treatments, supplements, and lifestyle changes, per public guides; labs or imaging are ordered at a local facility when needed, with follow-ups to fine-tune the plan.
- **Expanded visit types, per company releases:** urinary tract infections, bacterial vaginosis, and yeast infections, with same-day treatment including prescriptions when appropriate, and evaluation of underlying drivers such as genitourinary syndrome of menopause.
