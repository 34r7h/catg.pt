# Giftpack — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

Giftpack (giftpack.ai) positions itself as **global incentive infrastructure for enterprises**: a platform to run branded storefronts, custom merchandise, rewards/gift cards, automation and global fulfillment for gifting and incentive programs.

## What it is
"One platform for branded storefronts, custom merchandise, rewards, automation and global fulfillment." Giftpack says it helps companies "foster relationships, automate operations, boost retention and recurring revenue."

## Products and solutions (site categories)
- **Swag & custom merchandise** (/swags) — global swag factories, customizable branded items.
- **Gifting marketplace/catalog** (/browse) — global product catalog with gift cards and physical goods for programs across markets.
- **Personal gifting** (/personal-gifting) — smart gifting for individuals/groups, AI-assisted selection.
- **Sales & business incentives** (/sales-and-business) — rep incentives, channel/partner programs, SPIFFs, referrals/loyalty.
- **Employee appreciation/recognition** — highlighted across pages (team recognition features).
- **Government/education** (/gov) — program support for public sector.
- **Integration/automation** (/integration) — connect to workflows and systems; API available (/api).

## How it works (from /how-it-works)
Three core steps are implied: create a program/storefront, select recipients/products, automate and fulfill globally with tracking. The site emphasizes automation + global fulfillment.
