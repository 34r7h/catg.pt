# Phantom — distilled knowledge

Source: LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #32; phantom.com (official site, including its money-app announcement) and public wallet guides, accessed 2026-10-29. Pages skipped: none in this distillation — listing-plus-official-site distillation, full site capture pending.

## 1. What it is
**Phantom is a self-custody crypto wallet, originally built for Solana and now multi-chain.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #32.
- **Self-custody:** private keys stay with the user, encrypted on their device, not held by an exchange.
- **Networks named in public guides:** Solana, Ethereum, Base, Polygon, Sui, Bitcoin, and others — check phantom.com for the current supported list before transacting.
- **Forms:** browser extension (Chrome, Firefox, Brave, Edge) and mobile apps for iOS and Android.

## 2. What it does
- **Store, send, and receive:** hold crypto assets and NFTs, send and receive across supported networks.
- **Swap:** built-in token swaps without leaving the wallet.
- **Stake:** stake SOL directly in the wallet, per public guides.
- **Connect to apps:** an in-app browser on mobile and a browser-injected provider on desktop connect the wallet to decentralized apps.
- **Transaction safety:** transaction simulation before signing, human-readable transaction summaries, and phishing and malicious-program detection, per public wallet guides.
- **Money features (US, per phantom.com):** funding by bank transfer, direct deposit, Apple Pay, or debit card; wires; a virtual Phantom debit card usable anywhere Visa is accepted; and Cash balances usable at participating Stripe merchants. Identity verification is required for funding, wires, and the card.

## 3. Setting up safely
- **Download only from the official site:** phantom.com. Older guides also reference phantom.app; treat any other download source as suspect.
- **Recovery phrase:** a new wallet shows a 12-word secret recovery phrase. Write it on paper, store it offline, and never share it, screenshot it, or type it into a website.
- **Seedless option:** public guides describe login with Apple or Google plus a PIN as an alternative to a seed phrase.
- **Hardware:** Ledger hardware-wallet connection is supported, per public guides.
