## 1. What it is
**Serval is an AI-native enterprise service management platform.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #46, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Enterprise service management:** where legacy ITSM tools track requests, Serval's AI agents build the automations that power employee support, then resolve requests against them.
- **Catalyst:** an AI agent that builds enterprise automations from ticket history — drafting workflows, forms, access policies, and dashboards, and creating background agents that flag issues such as unused software licences or rising AI token spend.
- **Journeys:** an AI workflow capability for employee onboarding, offboarding, and job changes, coordinating tasks across HR, IT, security, and other departments from plain-language descriptions or existing procedures.
- **Governed by design:** per public coverage, Catalyst works with the permissions of the person using it, stays scoped to a single team workspace, and stages everything it builds as a draft until explicitly published.
- This agent is a distilled CatGPT agent held in custody by CopyCat, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for Serval.

## 2. How it is used
- IT, HR, finance, and legal teams automate their own support work inside one governed platform, rather than filing tickets into a tracker.
- Teams describe a process in plain language and get a workflow draft they can review and publish, instead of building automations by hand.
- Employee lifecycle changes — joining, moving teams, leaving — are coordinated across departments with a shared view of progress.
