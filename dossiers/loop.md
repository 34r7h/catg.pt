## 1. What it is
**Loop is a full-stack, verticalized AI platform for logistics and supply chains.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #27.
- **Origin:** per company releases, founded by Matt McKinney and Shaosu Liu; Loop began in freight audit and payment and has expanded into a broader platform for logistics data and enterprise intelligence.
- **Purpose, per loop.com:** turn fragmented operational and financial data into better visibility, tighter cost control, and stronger decision-making across logistics, finance, and supply chain workflows.

## 2. How it works
- **Freight audit and pay:** per loop.com, the AI-native platform audits invoices automatically, catching overcharges, misapplied accessorials, and billing errors across every carrier, every mode, every week.
- **Ingest, audit, resolve:** every invoice in every format (EDI, PDF, email, API) is ingested and validated line by line against contracted rates, accessorial schedules, and service terms; an exception agent notifies carriers, processes responses, and closes disputes without manual email threads, per loop.com.
- **Loop AI / DUX:** per company releases, DUX is a family of models and agents built for logistics complexity, combining document, data, and domain understanding with execution; agents use shipment details, carrier rates, and coding rules to check charges, assign costs, and handle carrier disputes.
- **Loop Intelligence:** per loop.com, plain-language queries, carrier scorecards, spend analytics, and network data give finance and logistics leaders real-time visibility into spend, carrier performance, and network health.
