## 1. What it is
**Zepto is an Indian quick-commerce platform delivering groceries and daily essentials from local dark stores.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #1, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Dark-store model:** small, densely located micro-warehouses stocked with fast-moving items, so most orders travel a short distance rather than from a large regional warehouse.
- **Range:** fresh groceries plus electronics, beauty essentials, apparel, toys, and more, per Zepto's own company descriptions; Zepto Café extends the range to prepared food items.
- **Speed, stated carefully:** Zepto built its brand on rapid delivery, but delivery times shown in-app are estimates that depend on dark-store proximity, stock, traffic, and rider availability — not a guaranteed deadline for every order. Recent public reporting notes Indian government guidance asking quick-commerce platforms to drop mandatory "10-minute" promises from advertising.

## 2. How it is used
- A customer opens the Zepto app, orders groceries or essentials, and a nearby dark store picks and dispatches the order.
- Demand forecasting pre-positions the items a neighbourhood is most likely to order, which is what makes short delivery windows possible.
- Check the app for current availability, delivery estimate, and pricing in your area before ordering — none are stated here.
