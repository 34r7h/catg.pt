## 1. What it is
**Lucidity is a multi-cloud storage management platform that automates block-storage optimization for enterprises, with no code changes to the applications using that storage.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #14, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Official site:** lucidity.cloud.
- **Positioning, in Lucidity's own terms:** a "NoOps" cloud storage solution — storage management that runs itself rather than being a manual, reactive chore.

## 2. What it does
- **Auto-Scaler:** Lucidity's multi-cloud auto-scaler observes block storage and autonomously rightsizes disks, per company descriptions — expanding storage before it runs out and shrinking over-provisioned disks, so teams neither pay for idle capacity nor face downtime from full disks.
- **Works across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud,** and sits on top of cloud-native block storage used for databases, application server disks, and Kubernetes, per public coverage.
- **Visibility and recommendations:** unified visibility into storage metrics, data-driven recommendations for disk tiering, and one-click, downtime-free changes, per Lucidity case-study material.
- **Who uses it:** IT, DevOps, and FinOps teams at large enterprises, per company descriptions.
