## 1. What it is
**Synctera is a Banking-as-a-Service (BaaS) platform that gives fintechs, brands, and developers everything they need to build and launch embedded finance products.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #2.
- **Not a bank:** Synctera is a technology platform, not a bank; all banking services are provided by its network of FDIC-insured partner banks, who hold the licenses and charters required to offer regulated financial products.
- **What it provides:** accounts, cards, and money movement, a network of sponsor bank partners, and built-in compliance and operational tooling, plus APIs and an operational console for compliance and oversight.
- **Who uses it:** fintechs building neobanks or financial apps, software companies adding embedded finance, developers building banking infrastructure, and community banks launching and managing sponsor banking programs.
- **Marketplace model:** Synctera matches compatible fintech builders with community banks, so builders can launch an MVP or scale without their own bank charter, and banks gain new revenue streams.
- **Business accounts:** the platform supports fintechs serving business customers, including interest-bearing deposit accounts, multiple debit card holders, spending limits, sub-accounts, and automated Know Your Business (KYB) verification.

## 2. How it is used
- A fintech connects through Synctera's APIs to offer accounts and cards under a partner bank's charter, with compliance workflows and an operational console for oversight.
- A community bank joins the network to run sponsor banking programs and manage fintech partnerships compliantly.
