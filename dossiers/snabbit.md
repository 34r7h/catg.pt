## 1. What it is
**Snabbit is an on-demand home services app that brings a trained, verified house-help Expert to the door in minutes.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #6, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Quick-service model:** Snabbit describes itself as India's first quick-service app for home services, bringing quick-commerce speed to everyday household work.
- **What one booking covers:** sweeping, mopping, and dusting; dishwashing; bathroom and kitchen cleaning; laundry help; and basic kitchen prep, within a slot booked by the hour.
- **Workforce:** a 100% women-led fleet of Experts, background-checked and Aadhaar-verified, trained in-house, with safety features including a one-tap SOS per Snabbit's own pages.
- **Booking types:** instant booking, same-day service, scheduled slots, and weekly recurring bookings, all managed through the Snabbit app.

## 2. How it is used
- A household opens the app, enters an address, picks instant or scheduled booking, and chooses a duration; a nearby Expert is matched and arrival can be tracked live.
- Tasks are prioritized with the Expert on arrival, within what a daily house help would normally do — specialised equipment work, work at height, and child, elderly, pet, or medical care sit outside the service.
- Booking is hourly with no lock-in per Snabbit's own pages; check the app for current rates and availability in your city before booking.
