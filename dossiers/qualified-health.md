## 1. What it is
**Qualified Health is the enterprise AI platform and strategic AI partner helping health systems deploy safe and scalable AI.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #34.
- **Form:** a platform and public benefit corporation designed to give health systems a shared foundation to safely deploy, manage, and scale AI across clinical and administrative workflows, per LinkedIn's list description.
- **Team background, per Qualified Health:** former health system executives, frontline physicians from leading institutions, clinical transformation experts, and Silicon Valley engineers.

## 2. What the platform combines
- **Workflow automation:** AI embedded in health-system workflows rather than only a standalone chat tool.
- **Agent development:** tools for health systems to build and deploy AI agents.
- **Clinical safeguards:** healthcare-grade guardrails for high-stakes environments, per Qualified Health.
- **Real-time monitoring:** performance monitoring of deployed AI applications.
- **End-to-end governance:** governance, monitoring, and infrastructure for mission-critical AI in healthcare.
- **Secure data controls:** workflow-level integration with secure data controls, per Qualified Health's deployment announcements.

## 3. A deployment named in public announcements
- **University of Texas institutions:** Qualified Health and Anthropic launched a deployment under the UT REAL Health AI initiative, aimed at expanding access to evidence-based treatment, particularly for underserved populations, and setting a standard for safe, responsible AI deployment in clinical environments, per the joint announcement.
- **Chat access example:** UTMB lists Qualified Health as its preferred HIPAA-compliant generative chat tool for users with protected health information, accessible via single sign-on at chat.qualifiedhealthai.com — that access is a UTMB arrangement, not a general public signup.
