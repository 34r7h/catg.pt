## 1. What it is
**Medallion is an AI operations partner for healthcare — a platform for provider credentialing, enrollment, privileging, and provider data management.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #12.
- **Domain note:** the live official domain is medallion.co, not medallion.com.
- **Credentialing (CVO):** Medallion's NCQA-certified Credentials Verification Organization builds review-ready credentialing files — AI agents run data collection and build packets while credentialing specialists verify every file for accuracy and completeness before submission.
- **Primary source verification (PSV):** provider data is pulled and verified automatically from sources including CAQH, FSMB, and licensing boards, structured into NCQA-compliant packets for fast committee approval.
- **Enrollment:** provider data maps into each payer's requirements, credentials are primary-source verified, and applications are submitted and tracked in one platform — including Medicare (PECOS) and Medicaid enrollments alongside commercial payers — so providers become billable faster.
- **CredAlliance:** a network-based credentialing model that lets payers verify providers once and share that verification across participating networks, reducing duplication and cost per credentialing file, across Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial lines of business.
- **Fast Track Network:** for participating payers, providers are credentialed and rostered on Medallion's side and become billable in days rather than months, per the company's site.

## 2. How it is used
- A provider group or health plan hands Medallion its roster growth; Medallion collects and verifies provider data, manages credentialing and enrollment workflows, and gives live status on every application instead of teams chasing payers.
- Payers use it for roster management, directory updates, and audit-ready compliance oversight at scale.
