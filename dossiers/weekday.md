## 1. What it is
**Weekday is an AI-powered hiring platform that brings startup roles into one place for job seekers and runs AI-native recruiting for employers.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #8, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **For job seekers:** an AI-powered platform that crawls jobs across the web and curates high-growth startup roles. Two kinds of listings appear: partnered roles, where applying via Weekday fast-tracks the application, and external roles, where candidates can "AI Apply" directly on the company's careers page.
- **Referrals in one click:** users opt in to giving referrals at their company, and can ask for referrals from thousands of opted-in referrers with one click.
- **For employers:** Weekday describes itself as a truly AI-native recruiting agency, using its own tooling to run sourcing, outreach, interview scheduling, and pipeline measurement faster than a conventional agency, with a focus on early-stage startups.

## 2. How it is used
- A job seeker searches Weekday's curated startup roles, applies via the platform or via AI Apply, and requests a referral where one is available.
- A startup uses Weekday to source candidates, coordinate interview rounds, check candidate intent before scheduling to avoid no-shows, and track funnel performance.
- Salary figures shown on Weekday are calculated from salaries shared by its own users in similar roles, per Weekday's own explanation — they are user-reported averages, not employer-published pay bands.
