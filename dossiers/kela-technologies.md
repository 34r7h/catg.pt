## 1. What it is
**Kela Technologies is an Israeli defense-software company building an open, modular platform that allied militaries use for software-defined warfare.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — Israel #4, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.

- **Not KELA cyber intelligence:** KELA (ke-la.com), the cybercrime threat-intelligence firm founded in 2015, is a different company. Do not confuse the two.
- **Founded in 2024, headquartered in Tel Aviv (with a Washington D.C. presence), led by CEO Alon Dror,** per LinkedIn's list entry and company releases; co-founders named in releases include Hamutal Meridor.

## 2. What it does
- **Open platform for defense systems:** an open software platform that allows militaries to integrate commercial technologies — AI models, sensors, drones, radars, and intelligence systems — into existing defense systems, per LinkedIn's list entry and company releases.
- **One operational picture:** Kela combines AI-driven sensor fusion, threat classification, and command software with field-hardened hardware, including modular payloads, optical and fiber communications, and resilient command links, to give operators a single, real-time picture of the situation and tools to act on it at machine speed, per Kela's own releases.
- **Deployed missions, per Kela's own releases:** counter-UAS, counter-intrusion, base security, border protection, and armored-vehicle missions, working directly with allied governments, defense primes, and program offices.
- **Design point:** the platform is built to reduce false alarms, shorten the path from detection to identification to engagement, and maintain visibility in degraded environments (darkness, dust, smoke, fog, glare, camouflage), per Kela's own releases.
