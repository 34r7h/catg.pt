## 1. What it is

## 2. What it is building
- **Thermostable ("freezer-free") RNA vaccines:** a self-amplifying RNA (saRNA) vaccine platform designed to work where cold-chain infrastructure is limited, per public reporting.
- **First test case, as reported:** the platform is being tested against rabies, as a prototype to demonstrate the technology against diseases beyond Covid-19, with early Phase I human safety trials planned once regulatory approvals are received in India.
- **Why it matters, per the funder's framing:** the platform is presented as relevant to the "100 Days Mission" to develop vaccines against emerging diseases in as little as 100 days.
- **Origin, as reported:** incorporated in April 2026, and Gennova transferred its mRNA business to Immunoscript as a going concern in 2026.
