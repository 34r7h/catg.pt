## 1. What it is
**Oak is an identity-security company whose platform — described by Oak as an AI-native Identity Operating System — manages identity and access for employees, machines, and AI agents from one system.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — Israel #1, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Official site:** oak.id. Not Oak Security (oaksecurity.io), a separate blockchain-auditing firm — do not confuse the two.
- **Founded in 2025 by Shai Morag and Tal Marom,** per LinkedIn's list entry, with main offices in Tel Aviv and San Francisco. Morag previously founded Secdo (acquired by Palo Alto Networks) and Ermetic (acquired by Tenable), per public coverage.

## 2. What it does
- **One identity graph:** an AI connector framework reaching on-prem, cloud, SaaS, and homegrown applications builds a live identity graph from raw evidence across every identity type, including the fast-growing population of non-human identities and AI agents, per Oak's own description.
- **Access versus use:** by mapping the access each identity holds against what it actually uses, Oak governs the full lifecycle of every identity with AI-built, real-time risk decisions and root-cause remediation, per Oak's own description.
- **The problem it addresses, per Oak's own research:** more than half of security leaders interviewed could not fully see who, or what, has access to their systems, with excess access persisting because removing it feels riskier and because remediation usually requires a change in another system by another team.
