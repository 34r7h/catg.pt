# NBRLY — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**NBRLY is a free, friendship-first social app for your neighborhood: drop a spontaneous "Signal" — coffee, drinks, a walk, a park hang — and see who nearby is free right now.** Positioning: "Your Neighborhood Front Porch — Make friends right on your block." Built for NYC first ("Built with love for NYC blocks & beyond"), 100% free, 18+.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Would-be user: "Never hang out alone — propose a spontaneous real-world plan and see who's down in minutes."
- Someone new to NYC: "Whether you're new to NYC, tired of doing everything alone, or just feel like meeting someone new tonight, NBRLY helps you get out there."
- Anyone assuming it's a dating app: "It's friendship-first: not dating, no swiping, no endless messaging — real people, real plans, right now."

## How it works (site + App Store)
1. **See** people and activities nearby (interactive block map / feed).
2. **Drop a Signal** — set an activity, pick a nearby spot, head out; nearby neighbors with matching vibes are notified instantly.
3. **Connect** with people who are available now; meet up somewhere public.
4. **Make a new friend** — "Turn 'we should hang out sometime' into actually hanging out."

## Signals (the core mechanic)
"Drop a Signal. Connect in Minutes." — propose spontaneous real-world activities and see who's down right now. "No endless planning, just good company around the corner." Signal types on the site:
- **Coffee Run** (~15–30 min) — "Grabbing an afternoon cortado or working from a local café?"
- **Evening Drinks** (Tonight) — neighborhood wine bar or brewery after work.
- **Grab a Bite** (Casual) — dumplings, weekend bagels, late-night pizza: "neighbor foodies who never want to eat alone."
- **Neighborhood Stroll** (Walking) — dog walks, tree-lined blocks.
- **Park Hangout** (Sunny Days) — lawn blankets, frisbees, good books.
- **Spontaneous Hang** (Open Ended) — thrifting, pickup basketball, museum visits, board games: "Propose anything on your mind."

## Three feature pillars (site "How It Works")
1. **Community First — "Your Neighborhood Front Porch":** share genuine thoughts and updates with your immediate block; "No algorithms burying local voices or feeding rage bait"; discover local recommendations and tips from neighbors.
2. **Spontaneous Signals — "Never Hang Out Alone":** "Most apps trap you on a screen. NBRLY is built to get you outside in the real world." Low friction (set activity → pick nearby spot → head out); zero pressure, friendly, spontaneous.
3. **Gatherings & Events — "Host or Find Local Gatherings":** rooftop mixers, weekend run clubs, board game nights, flea markets. Explore upcoming happenings on an **interactive block map**; host your own gathering with **simple RSVP & guest limits**; **temporary group chats created automatically for logistics**.
