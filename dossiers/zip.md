## 1. What it is
**Zip is an AI platform for enterprise procurement — procurement orchestration with full intake-to-pay capabilities, replacing fragmented tools and manual processes with intelligent orchestration.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #24.
- **Not to confuse with:** Zip Co, the Australian buy-now-pay-later company (zip.co); this entry is the enterprise procurement platform at ziphq.com. Note also that zip.com pages serve Zip procurement content; ziphq.com is the domain cited in the company's own announcements.
- **Intake-to-procure:** a single intake portal where every purchase and vendor request begins — one standardized form, routed automatically to finance, IT, legal, or other approvers based on company rules — so policy is enforced at the start and maverick spend is prevented.
- **Custom approval workflows:** dynamic, no-code workflows that adapt to each request, routing by spend, vendor risk, or department, with parallel review instead of sequential email chains.
- **Vendor management and onboarding:** centralized supplier data as a single source of truth, automated onboarding with parallel legal, compliance, and security reviews, contract tracking, renewal alerts, and supplier-risk flags.
- **AI agents:** Zip describes itself as having the most widely deployed AI agents in enterprise procurement, with AI-driven analysis and recommendations for legal, risk, and financial approvals; it can work as a full source-to-pay platform or as the intake and orchestration layer over suites like Coupa and SAP Ariba, syncing bi-directionally with ERP systems including SAP, Oracle Fusion, NetSuite, and Microsoft Dynamics 365.

## 2. How it is used
- An employee submits a purchase or vendor request through the intake portal; it flows through approvals, sourcing, contracts, and invoicing with visibility at every step, instead of starting in email or spreadsheets.
- Procurement teams gain early spend visibility and can demonstrate efficiency gains and cost savings from upstream controls.
