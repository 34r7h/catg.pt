## 1. What it is
**AfterQuery is an applied research lab curating data solutions for frontier foundation model development.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #31.
- **The problem, in AfterQuery's own framing:** today's models can generate answers but struggle with real work, because real work is decisions, tradeoffs, and context — knowledge that lives inside experts, not on the internet.
- **The answer:** work with domain experts to capture how they think, then structure that thinking into training data models can learn from.
- **Who it serves:** frontier AI research labs and enterprises building advanced models, per afterquery.com.

## 2. What it builds
- **Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT):** high-quality prompt–response pairs and chain-of-thought reasoning traces, teaching models how to behave across complex tasks.
- **Reinforcement Learning + Rubrics:** expert-designed prompts with grading frameworks for reasoning and code generation, turning subjective judgment into scalable reward signals.
- **Agent Environments (API / MCP):** custom environments across APIs, tools, and services, enabling training and evaluation of agents in real workflows.
- **Computer Use Trajectories:** human-demonstrated interactions across browser and desktop environments, teaching models to navigate and operate software end to end.
- **Sectors named on afterquery.com and in its Y Combinator profile:** finance, software engineering, medicine, and law, among other professional domains.

## 3. How it differs from generic data labeling
- **Process over answers:** AfterQuery's stated distinction is training on how experts actually solve problems — step by step, decision by decision — rather than only on final outputs.
- **Experts, not just annotators:** datasets are built with verified practicing professionals, per AfterQuery's own materials.
- **Benchmarks:** custom benchmarks are part of the offer, alongside fine-tuning data and human-led reinforcement learning data.
