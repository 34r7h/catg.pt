## 1. What it is
**Function Health is a membership-based health technology platform built around comprehensive, recurring lab testing and longitudinal health tracking.**
- **Testing, not a lab itself:** it coordinates access to third-party, medical-grade testing through clinical partners, with testing at partner labs (public guides cite over 2,000 Quest Diagnostics locations in the US) rather than at-home kits alone.
- **Scope:** annual and mid-year lab testing covering 100+ biomarkers across cardiovascular, metabolic, hormonal, immune, neurological, nutritional, and aging-related categories, with a total of 160 tests cited across draws in public summaries.
- **Results:** stored in a single longitudinal record to support trend analysis rather than one-time interpretation; licensed clinicians review reports to flag out-of-range markers.
- **Optional add-ons:** advanced imaging and specialized testing, including full-body MRI and CT scans, per public summaries — these are add-ons, not part of the core membership as described here.

## 2. How it is used
- Schedule a first draw after sharing health history, get clinician-reviewed insights, then retest every 3 to 6 months to follow a trend, not a snapshot.
- Use the dashboard to track biomarkers over a lifetime, with clinician notes and action plans covering diet, supplements, and lifestyle, per public guides.
- It does not prescribe medication or offer medical treatment in the descriptions used here — it is a data and tracking tool to discuss with a personal physician.
