## 1. What it is
**PALMONAS is an Indian demi-fine jewellery brand selling design-led, everyday-wear pieces at accessible price points.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #10, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Demi-fine, defined:** jewellery that sits between fast fashion and traditional fine jewellery — gold-plated, silver, platinum-plated silver, and 9KT gold pieces designed for daily wear.
- **Product promise:** per the brand's own descriptions, waterproof, skin-safe, design-led pieces, including collections such as everyday gold jewellery, 9KT lab-grown diamond pendants, studs, rings, and bracelets.
- **Channels:** online at palmonas.com and through physical stores across India, with an international debut in the UAE per public coverage.
- **Founding, as reported:** founded in 2022 by Pallavi Mohadikar and Amol Patwari, with Shraddha Kapoor joining as co-founder in March 2024 per investor announcements.

## 2. How it is used
- A shopper browses collections online or in store, buys a piece for everyday wear rather than for a single occasion, and wears it daily — the brand's positioning is jewellery for the wearer herself, not only for occasions.
- Collections are built around themes and symbols — for example, an Evil Eye collection drawing on cultural belief — alongside minimal stackables and statement pieces.
