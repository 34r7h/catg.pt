## 1. What it is
**Higgsfield is an all-in-one platform for professional video and image production, built around AI filmmaking.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #38 (listed as "Higgsfield" on LinkedIn).
- **Mission, per higgsfield.ai:** make cinematic-quality video and image creation accessible to anyone with an idea, from first-time creators to professional filmmakers and Fortune 500 agencies.
- **What it is not:** a single prompt-to-clip generator alone. Its pitch is a production environment where camera, lens, lighting, pacing, and character performance are directed before generation.

## 2. The tools, per higgsfield.ai
- **Cinema Studio:** the AI filmmaking workspace for directing camera, lens, lighting, pacing, and character performance before generation. Version 4.0, announced October 2026, builds multi-shot scenes from up to 50 references, with a Director's Panel bringing shoot decisions into the generation process, and lets creators continue a shot or fix one detail without rebuilding the entire clip.
- **Soul models:** Soul 2.0 (photorealistic image generation for fashion and editorial) and Soul Cinema (cinematic-grade image generation), per higgsfield.ai.
- **Higgsfield DOP:** cinematic video generation.
- **Keyframes:** AI storyboard generation.
- **Character and color control:** Soul ID for character consistency and Soul HEX for color control, per higgsfield.ai.
- **Content Factory:** turns a "What if…" idea into a faceless video — script, characters, visuals, voiceover, and edit in one process, with videos from 15 seconds to 5 minutes, per higgsfield.ai.
- **Reasoning engine:** a proprietary engine that orchestrates in-house and partner models, planning narrative structure, camera logic, and visual consistency before generation, per higgsfield.ai.
