## 1. What it is
**Unconventional AI is developing a new computational substrate specifically for AI, aiming for biology-scale energy efficiency.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #43.
- **The problem, per Unconventional AI:** neural networks run on deterministic abstractions that execute on analog circuitry tuned to emulate digital behavior — many inefficiencies result, and at current growth rates there will not be enough energy to sustain AI workload growth.
- **The thesis:** instead of simulating a physical system, run the neural network on the physics directly — provide a software interface to the inherent physics of the silicon.
- **The goal, in the company's words:** capabilities far surpassing current models while consuming a mere fraction of the energy, with the human brain's roughly 20-watt budget as the reference point.
- **Co-founders, per the company:** CEO Naveen Rao (previously founded Nervana Systems, acquired by Intel, and MosaicML, acquired by Databricks), MIT Associate Professor Michael Carbin, Stanford Assistant Professor Sara Achour, and former Google engineer MeeLan Lee.

## 2. How it differs from "neuromorphic"
- **Rao's own distinction:** the company is not neuromorphic in the usual sense. It extracts principles of how and why computation in biology is so efficient and applies them to engineered systems — building a new kind of computer from first principles, focused on AI rather than deterministic calculation.
- **Dynamical systems approach, per Reuters:** simple computing elements interact with simple rules and evolve over time to reach a result, rather than following programmed step-by-step logic as conventional chips do.
- **Codesign:** the effort is as much an algorithmic and software challenge as a novel hardware design one — extreme hardware-software codesign is paramount, per the company.

## 3. Where it stands, per public reporting
- **First silicon:** by October 2026 the company had developed its first test chip and was nearing completion of its second, per Reuters — faster than its original research timeline.
- **NeuReality acquisition (October 2026, per Reuters):** Unconventional AI bought Israeli chip startup NeuReality in a cash-and-stock deal (sum undisclosed) for an engineering team with experience delivering finished products.
- **Target, per Reuters:** AI chips 1,000 times more energy efficient than current offerings — a company goal, not a measured result.
