## 1. What it is
**Parallel Web Systems builds web search and research APIs purpose-built for AI agents and agentic workflows.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #36.
- **Founded 2023, headquartered in Palo Alto, California,** co-founded by Parag Agrawal (former CEO of Twitter, now X) and Travers Nisbet, per public profiles.
- **Core idea:** large language models are trained on past information, so their knowledge is out of date as soon as training ends. Parallel grounds agents in current public web data.
- **Difference from general search APIs, per parallel.ai:** most search APIs retrofit traditional search engines for AI; Parallel's index was designed for LLMs and programmatic use from the start, returning information-dense excerpts optimized for a model's context window rather than ranked lists of links for humans.

## 2. The product suite, per parallel.ai
- **Search:** real-time retrieval returning ranked URLs with compressed, token-dense excerpts for direct inclusion in a model's context.
- **Extract:** convert a specific public URL — including JavaScript-heavy pages and hosted PDFs — into clean, structured text.
- **Task:** longer, multi-step research jobs synthesizing information across many sources, returning results with citations and confidence scores.
- **FindAll:** build structured datasets — lists of people, companies, or events — from a natural-language description.
- **Monitor:** watch specified parts of the web continuously and send webhook alerts when something changes.
- **Responses:** return a synthesized, cited answer to a question in seconds, positioned as an OpenAI-compatible drop-in for grounded question-answering.
- **Index:** Parallel runs its own web-scale index, with billions of pages and millions more added and updated daily, per parallel.ai.

## 3. Who uses it
- **Customers named by Parallel:** Harvey, Notion, Opendoor, Granola, and Dropbox, among others, plus Fortune 500 enterprises and leading AI companies including Attio and Modal, per Parallel announcements.
