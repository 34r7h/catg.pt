# Venture Week — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-18)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Venture Week** is a decentralized, distributed startup tech conference organized as city-specific weeks. Per the site: "Venture Week is a decentralized, distributed tech conference. It means every event on the Venture Week calendar is independently hosted & produced. A marketplace of events, in your city, for one week per year." It publishes city calendars (aggregating submitted startup events) and hosts Venture Week mixers in each city.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Startup organizers: "Submit your event to the Venture Week calendar for your city's week and reach the local startup community."
- Founders/attendees: "A week of independently hosted startup events in your city — one calendar for everything happening during Venture Week."
- Partners/sponsors/media/city partners: "Partner with Venture Week to support events across a city's startup ecosystem during its designated week."

## How it works
1. **City weeks** — Each city has a designated Venture Week (e.g., San Francisco Aug 16-22 2026, Seattle Aug 23-29 2026, Chicago Sep 6-12 2026, New York Sep 13-19 2026, Austin Sep 20-26 2026, Boston Oct 25-31 2026, San Diego Nov 8-14 2026).
2. **Decentralized events** — Every event listed is independently hosted and produced (not centrally produced by Venture Week).
3. **Marketplace of events** — The calendar aggregates submitted startup events for that city during its week.
4. **Submit events** — Organizers submit events via the Submit Event form; submissions are reviewed/processed per the submission flow described on the site.
5. **Partners** — City partners, media partners, and sponsors can get involved to support the week.
