## 1. What it is
**Port is an internal developer portal platform that lets platform engineering teams build a context-rich software catalog with maturity and quality scorecards, plus developer self-service actions and workflow automation.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — Israel #10.
- **Domain note:** the live product domain is getport.io (API at api.getport.io, app at app.getport.io, docs at docs.getport.io); some directories also list port.io, which is not used here as the product domain.
- **Software catalog:** centralizes information about services, cloud resources, environments, and tools, so developers can find dependencies, APIs, owners, and standards in one place instead of chasing tribal knowledge.
- **Self-service actions:** developers create, delete, provision, and modify resources with a few clicks — scaffolding a service, deploying an environment, managing secrets, toggling flags — with guardrails, rather than raising DevOps tickets.
- **Scorecards:** maturity and quality scorecards track services against organizational standards.
- **API-first:** Port ingests data from CI/CD systems, Git repositories, Kubernetes, and other sources, and is loosely coupled with whichever orchestrator a customer uses.

## 2. How it is used
- Platform teams define blueprints for their entities, connect integrations, and publish self-service actions; developers then plan, build, ship, and operate from the portal.
- A free version without user or time limits is offered per third-party platform directories; check getport.io for current plans before quoting pricing.
