## 1. What it is
**doubleAI is an Israeli AI company building "Code Execution Models" — AI that optimises software while it runs, rather than AI that writes code for a programmer.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — Israel #2, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.

## 2. What it does
- **Code Execution Models (CEMs):** a CEM lives inside the running program, between code and silicon. While coding agents act on behalf of the user and produce source code, a CEM acts on behalf of the hardware: its inputs are the running program, the hardware it runs on, and the data flowing through it, and its outputs are instructions meant directly for the machine, per doubleAI's own description.
- **Maxwell, the first CEM:** continuously inspects and improves the binary under the code as it runs, fitting it to the exact hardware, data, and usage encountered, on both GPUs and CPUs, while leaving the source code unchanged, per doubleAI's own description.
- **Verification claim:** every improvement Maxwell makes is formally and mathematically proven to leave the program's behaviour unchanged, per doubleAI's own description — treat this as a doubleAI claim, not an independently verified property.
- **Demonstrations, as reported by doubleAI:** Maxwell rewrote Nvidia's open-source cuGraph library, speeding up 55% of its algorithms by a factor of two or more, and improved the pandas toolkit's PDS-H benchmark performance by a factor of 38, per public coverage of the launch. These are company demonstrations, not independent benchmarks.
