## 1. What it is
**Swish is a 10-minute food delivery platform in India that owns its kitchens, technology, and last-mile delivery, rather than aggregating restaurant kitchens it does not own.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #11, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Official site:** justswish.in — not swish.com, which is a different service.
- **Model, in Swish's own framing:** neighbourhood kitchens with dedicated preparation, cooking, and assembly stations, serving customers close by so food arrives in around 10 minutes.
- **Founded in 2024 by Aniket Shah, Ujjwal Sukheja, and Saran S,** per public coverage, headquartered in Bengaluru.

## 2. How it is used
- A customer orders snacks, beverages, or meals in the Swish app and a nearby Swish kitchen prepares and delivers the order, rather than a restaurant dispatching it through a marketplace.
- The menu, per public coverage, spans more than 250 items across more than 20 food categories, with lunch and dinner as the largest occasions.
- Cities named in public coverage include Bengaluru and Delhi-NCR (Gurugram, Noida, Delhi, Ghaziabad).
