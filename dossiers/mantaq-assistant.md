# Mantaq — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Mantaq is an AI automation & software studio for teams that run on paperwork.** Its formula: "Forms in. A person reviews. Documents out. Every step on record." Documents arrive in any format, the right person checks them, the finished document goes out — and every step is logged so you can prove it later.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Regulated team (healthcare/compliance): "HIPAA-grade document workflows where a human confirms every AI reading — three layers built by one team."
- Ops/HR lead: "Twenty minutes per letter becomes twenty seconds, and the audit trail is built in."
- Nonprofit/grants: "The receipt is captured once; the reconciliation packet comes out already in the format your funder accepts."

## How it works (their pattern)
1. Documents arrive in any format (structured, scanned, or a sideways photo) → the system reads them.
2. The right person reviews — role-based screens; nothing goes out until someone signs off.
3. Finished documents come out (letters, signed packs, packets), connected to the tools you already use.
4. Every step is recorded so it can be proved later.

## Three ways to start ("start where you actually are")
- **Audit — 48 hours.** Map the work before anyone builds anything: which documents arrive and how many a week, how many hands touch each one, what gets retyped and where it stalls → a build plan you keep, whether or not you hire Mantaq.
- **Build.** The system itself: document intake and reading, review screens per role, documents out and signed, full record, connected to your tools. **The client owns it** — code goes to their accounts, their infrastructure, in the contract before work starts.
- **Run.** After launch: monitoring and uptime, new document types, edge cases, changes as rules change.

## What they build (three layers, one team)
- **Documents & Automation (the engine):** reading documents (any format/angle), signatures (sent, tracked, stored), integrations into existing systems, human oversight.
- **Platform & Development (the body):** screens per role, web applications, mobile applications, internal tools, connections to the CRM/records/payroll you already pay for.
- **Interface & Design (the soul):** screen design, role-based review dashboards, mobile screens, design system, accessibility — "if your team won't use it, you bought nothing."

## Positioning (objections, from the site's FAQ)
- **"You're in Pakistan — how does that work for a US client?"** → calls run the client's hours (overlapping their morning); the rest is async, so progress arrives overnight. Most clients are in the US, UK and EU, many on their second or third project.
- **"We handle medical records. HIPAA?"** → yes, and they have (VA Claims Made Easy is HIPAA-compliant on AWS, custom login, encrypted storage, a person checking everything). They check which rules apply before quoting — including ones not mentioned (substance-use records carry a second rule that changes the design).
- **"Who owns what you build?"** → the client does. Code in the client's accounts, on their infrastructure, in the contract before work starts; "we don't license your own system back to you."
- **"Do we replace our existing tools?"** → almost never. Most builds sit between tools the client already pays for (PeopleWorks kept Pipefy); the audit tells what to keep before what to add.
