# Synced — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-18)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Synced is an AI inbox that finds and completes work across your messages.** It reads email and messages, finds files, checks calendars, drafts responses, turns messages into tasks, and coordinates schedules — but nothing sends without your approval. It's in private beta (web + iOS).

**One line for different audiences:**
- Founder: "Your inbox on autopilot — drafts replies, books meetings, pulls files; you approve before sending."
- EA/sales/recruiter: "Handles busywork across email/messages, calendar, and files with human-in-the-loop approval."
- Knowledge worker: "Turns messages into actionable work (tasks) and coordinates next steps without app-switching."

## How it works (high level)
- **Read & understand** — Parses email/messages to identify work
- **Gather context** — Searches email, files, cloud storage, calendar
- **Draft & propose** — Writes replies, creates documents, proposes meetings, creates tasks
- **Human-in-the-loop** — "Nothing sends without you" — all AI-drafted messages wait for approval
- **Execute on approval** — Handles follow-ups and tracks progress
