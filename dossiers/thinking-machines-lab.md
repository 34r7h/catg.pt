## 1. What it is
**Thinking Machines Lab is an AI research and product company founded in February 2025 by Mira Murati, former chief technology officer of OpenAI.**
- **Focus:** building AI systems that encode human values, adapt to a broader range of human professional domains, and collaborate with humans, rather than fully automated systems alone, per the company's launch statements.
- **Team:** about 30 researchers and engineers at launch, including former OpenAI researchers, with John Schulman (OpenAI co-founder) as chief scientist and Barret Zoph as technology chief at founding, per public reporting.
- **First product, Tinker:** launched in October 2025 as an API that lets researchers fine-tune frontier open-weight models without managing the underlying compute, per public reporting. Early users cited include Princeton, Stanford, and Berkeley.
- **Later model work:** public reporting in 2026 cites an open-weights model called Inkling, released in July 2026 — this is third-party reporting, not a company site fact in this context, and should be labeled as such if cited.

## 2. How it is used
- Researchers use Tinker to fine-tune open-weight models for their own work, without running their own training infrastructure.
- The lab's stated direction is multimodal systems that work across text, audio, and video and collaborate with people.
