## 1. What it is
**Matiks is a mobile gaming app that turns math, memory, logic, and puzzle play into competitive social games.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #3, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **The pitch:** mental math treated like a sport — an alternative to algorithmic feeds, positioned closer to competitive gaming than to a conventional study app.
- **How play works:** players practise against bots, then enter real-time 1v1 duels and multiplayer matchups, with daily challenges, puzzles, leaderboards, streaks, points, and progress tracking.
- **Creator direction:** per third-party reporting, Matiks is building Jelo, an experimental platform that lets users build their own games, and an AI creation engine for user-built games.
- **Founding, as reported:** founded in 2024 by Sudhanshu Bhatia, Mohan Kumar, and Sushant Timmapur, based in Bengaluru.

## 2. How it is used
- A player opens the app, picks a category — math, puzzle, memory, or logic — and plays a quick duel or challenge.
- Streaks, points, and unlockable games provide progression; community features let players coach each other and run their own groups.
- The app is aimed at players who want active mental play rather than passive scrolling.
