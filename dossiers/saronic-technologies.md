## 1. What it is

- **Mission, per Saronic:** redefine maritime superiority by integrating ASVs into traditional naval and maritime fleets, enabling operators to go farther and do more with less risk to life and mission.
- **Headquartered in Austin, Texas,** with hardware, software, and manufacturing teams working side by side, per Saronic.
- **Approach:** vertically integrated platforms that are highly interoperable, modular, and adaptable to evolving mission requirements, engineered for rapid production at scale.

## 2. The vessel family, per saronic.com
- **Corsair (24'):** an ASV capable of carrying up to 1,000 lbs over 1,000 nautical miles, at 35+ knots top speed — built to deliver multi-mission capabilities rapidly and at scale.
- **Mirage (52'):** an ASV with 2,500+ nautical miles of range and 3,500 lbs of payload capacity, at 35+ knots top speed — a force multiplier for manned and unmanned teams in every ocean.
- **Marauder (180'):** Saronic's premier platform for logistics and at-sea payload deployment, carrying up to 150 metric tons in modular configurations (four 40' ISO containers, eight 20' ISO containers, or other payloads), with 5,400 nautical miles of range at base load, 12 knots cruise, and 25+ knots sprint, per saronic.com.

## 3. What the vessels are for
- **Naval augmentation:** small, autonomous boats that augment larger naval vessels, extending range, capability, and survivability without putting crews at risk.
- **Mission types named in public coverage:** reconnaissance, surveillance, electronic warfare, logistics, and at-sea payload deployment.
- **Positioning and navigation:** Saronic's vessels use GNSS/INS with anti-jamming and anti-spoofing resilience for operations under interference, per a Saronic partner case study.
- **Manufacturing scale:** Saronic is expanding shipyard capacity, including a Franklin, Louisiana shipyard investment reported in 2025, to produce ASVs at the speed and scale of mission relevance, per regional economic-development announcements.
