## 1. What it is
**CareYaya Health Technologies is a health technology platform that connects families with student caregivers for older adults.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #47, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Student caregiver model:** caregivers are pre-health college students, about a third holding CNA certifications per CareYaya's own FAQ summary; students choose appointments that fit their schedules and gain paid patient-care experience.
- **Companion and daily-living care:** companionship and help with daily activities — mobility, grooming, meals, technology, light housekeeping, pet care — including overnight and 24/7 care arrangements.
- **What it is not:** per CareYaya's own FAQ, student caregivers are not professionally trained caregivers and do not provide bathing/showering help, catheter or tracheostomy care, injections, IV care, lift-device use, tube feeding, medication administration (reminders only), or wound care.
- **Research angle:** CareYaya describes itself as an applied research lab, working with research institutions on caregiving, AI, and related support for aging and seriously ill populations.

## 2. How it is used
- A family requests care on careyaya.org, describes the frequency and level of care sought, and schedules an onboarding call; once verified, they can send care requests to caregivers on the platform.
- A student caregiver applies online, browses open appointments with time, pay, and location details, and accepts or declines each one.
- Care is positioned as a supplement to, not a replacement for, agency or hospice services for medical needs.
