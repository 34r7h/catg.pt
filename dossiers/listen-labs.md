## 1. What it is
**Listen Labs is an end-to-end AI platform for customer understanding.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #24.
- **What it does, per the company:** Listen Labs studies show companies what their customers think, why they think it, and what to do about it.
- **AI-moderated interviews at scale:** per public guides, AI conducts and moderates qualitative interviews, transcribes, synthesizes, and surfaces themes — scaling qualitative studies to hundreds or thousands of participants that would be impractical to interview manually.

## 2. How it works
- **Interviews, not just surveys:** open-ended, conversational responses give richer, more nuanced insight than surveys alone, per public guides, helping product, marketing, and UX teams make decisions grounded in real customer voices.
- **Listen Twins, per the 2026-10-27 company release:** AI models that predict how real customers behave, built from millions of interviews and "Keystone Questions" that unlock how people actually behave — used for questions such as what customers would pay more for, or how people in another country would respond before a market entry.
- **Go/no-go caution, per the same release:** for higher-stakes go/no-go decisions, Listen Labs continues to recommend a full research study using its AI-moderation product rather than a Twins prediction alone.
- **Scale claims are company-stated:** per the company release, more than 2 million AI-powered interviews across a global network of 50+ million participants; treat these as company figures, not independently verified counts.
