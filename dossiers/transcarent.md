## 1. What it is
**Transcarent is a health and care platform — "One Place for Health and Care" — that puts benefits navigation, clinical guidance, and care delivery in one place for members, employers, health plans, and health systems.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #36.
- **WayFinding:** the member experience that combines generative AI with instant access to clinical guidance, benefits navigation, and virtual or in-person care delivery on one platform, with human support never more than a tap away — members "Just Ask Transcarent" instead of sorting through a deluge of confusing benefits information.
- **Care Experiences:** WayFinding plus condition-specific programs — Muscle and Joint Health (with a Centers of Excellence network), Cancer Care (prevention to survivorship), Weight Health (connected, on-demand clinical, lifestyle, and behavior-change support), Pharmacy Care (manage and shop for prescriptions in the app), and Mental Health (therapy and psychiatry from home).
- **Aligned incentives:** Transcarent is aligned with those who pay for healthcare — self-insured employers, health consumers, and supporting payors — and offers at-risk pricing models and transparent impact reporting, per the company's site.
- **Scale, per the company:** more than 1,700 employers, health plans, and health systems relying on the platform for 21 million members, a company figure, not an independently verified count.
- **Accolade:** Transcarent acquired Accolade, the personalized healthcare navigation company, with stockholders approving the merger in 2025, per public merger coverage.

## 2. How it is used
- A member asks a question in the app and gets clinical guidance, benefits answers, and a next step to care — virtual primary care, a specialist, a pharmacy order, or a condition program — with single sign-on across point solutions.
- Employers offer Transcarent as the front door to their health benefits, replacing a fragmented set of point solutions members rarely use.
