## 1. What it is
**Drata is a compliance automation platform that automates and accelerates compliance — continuous, automated monitoring of a company's compliance posture.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #30.
- **Frameworks:** 25+ frameworks including SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR, and PCI, with multi-framework automation so one set of controls maps across frameworks.
- **Automated evidence collection:** documentation collected from the tech stack through hundreds of integrations and an open API, instead of manual screenshots and spreadsheets.
- **Continuous compliance:** monitoring of 100+ security controls, with tests kept accurate in collaboration with auditors and security experts, and real-time alerts and insights so issues surface early.
- **Vendor management:** streamlined vendor security questionnaires — store, send, and review answers.
- **Security policies:** 20+ customizable, auditor-approved policies, with streamlined employee signing and documentation.
- **Expert assistance:** step-by-step guidance from a compliance team, 24/5, per the company's site.

## 2. How it is used
- A company connects Drata to its cloud providers, identity management, and development tools; Drata collects evidence, monitors controls continuously, and generates compliance reports to share with stakeholders and auditors.
- Teams preparing for a SOC 2 audit use it to stay audit-ready year-round rather than scrambling before the audit window.
