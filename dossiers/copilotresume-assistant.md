# CopilotResume — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**CopilotResume (copilotresume.io) is an EU-hosted AI resume & CV builder with job tailoring, ATS checks and a job tracker.** You build a resume free (all templates), scan it against a real job ad for an ATS score and missing keywords, get an honest "should I apply?" verdict and a recruiter's-eye read, and export a PDF — first PDF export free. Made by a small Dutch company (ETLDataCompany B.V., Den Haag).

**One line for different audiences:**
- Job seeker: "Build free, get an ATS score against the real job ad, fix weak bullets with AI, track every application — first PDF free."
- Non-native English speakers / international seekers: data stored in the EU, never sold, never used to train AI; toolkit in 16 languages; country-specific formats (Canada, Germany, India, Netherlands, Philippines, South Africa…).
- Coaches / recruiters: site has dedicated pages (/resume-builder-for-coaches, /resume-builder-for-recruiters — linked, not in crawl).

## How it works (4 tools + tracker)
1. **ATS Check** — scores your resume against the real job ad, lists matched and missing keywords.
2. **Verdict** — honest "should I apply?" answer: strengths, gaps, what to do next.
3. **Darpan** — how a recruiter sees your CV: first impression, red flags, what you're worth.
4. **AI Coach** — rewrites a weak bullet into a result-driven one, in your own words and language.
Plus **Job Tracker** (free board): one row per job next to the exact resume used, ATS score saved with it, stages Applying → Interview prep, dated interviews/notes, outcome (offer / rejected / ghosted).
User paths on the homepage: "Ready for a change?" / "Chasing a dream job?" (ATS Check & Verdict → fix with AI Coach) / "Not sure what's going wrong?" (Darpan) / "Want feedback from someone you know?" (Share Live Resume + comments).
Free ATS scan flow: import resume (PDF/DOCX/TXT, "Stored in the EU · never used to train AI") → add job description → scan.

## Positioning / objections (site's own comparison table)
- **vs ChatGPT** — ChatGPT fine for wording a few lines; CopilotResume if you want the finished resume: ATS-safe templates, score vs the real job ad with missing keywords, one-click PDF.
- **vs Zety** — Zety fine if you already have a working resume there; CopilotResume if you want EU data storage (never sold, never used to train AI) and 16 supported languages.
- **vs resume.io** — resume.io fine if their plan suits you better; CopilotResume: build and check free, then $7 day pass / $15 30-day once (nothing renews) / $12 a month.
- **vs Kickresume / FlowCV** — those fine if you already have a resume you love; CopilotResume: AI that sharpens your real experience and never invents any, Darpan recruiter read, true two-column layouts, country-specific designs, free template/layout control.
- **Honest weak spot (stated on site):** the Job Tracker "is built for a laptop or desktop screen" — a phone-first tracker suits phone-only users better.
- Comparison caveat: "We only describe ourselves; other companies change their plans too often for us to speak for them."
