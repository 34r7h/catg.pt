# Omi — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Omi (by Based Hardware, San Francisco) is a wearable AI notetaker pendant ("your personal AI that remembers every conversation") plus desktop/phone/web software.** It captures what you say and do, turning it into summaries, tasks and searchable memory. Open source: software and hardware can run locally if you want. Tagline: "Your personal AI that remembers every conversation."

## Product
- **Omi pendant** — wearable AI notetaker that is always listening; captures conversations, then turns them into summaries, tasks and searchable memory. $129 (site price). "One memory. Two ways to wear it" — pendant on cord, a clip, or wrist band. Continuously captures (unlike a phone that stops when a call comes in, storage fills, or screen locks).
- **omi Glass** — open-source developer-focused smart glasses ("Omi Glass Dev Kit", $499 / list $799) that capture photos for lifelogging and AI-powered memory recall; hardware, firmware and software all open source (repo path `omi/OmiGlass` in github.com/BasedHardware/omi); 6× the battery life vs prior; fully hackable. Developer Kit & Brain Interface option.
- **Accessories** — wrist band ($59.99), charger ($29.99/$49.99), cords, etc. (collection page).
- **Apps** — desktop (Mac), mobile, web (app.omi.me) share the same memory ("The same memory on your Mac, your phone and the web").

## Manifesto / vision
"The next AI moat isn't intelligence. It's personal context." People choose the AI that *knows* them. Today's AI is "a vending machine: prompt → answer → forget." Omi is "your personal AI mentor" — it learns your life from what you say, do, and work on; it nudges, pushes, and keeps you honest. Belief: "Personal context beats raw IQ — a smaller model with your history beats a bigger model with none. The killer feature isn't chat. It's proactivity."
- **"How we win":** collect all data about the user; they'll trust it because Omi is open source; use the data for maximum personalization. "We use open source as a trust wedge."
- **Mission:** "Make humans 1,000× more productive."
- **Roadmap (four phases, one personal AI):**
  - Phase 1 · now — **Desktop software**: start where life happens (computer); capture meetings (what you said), files (who you are), screen (what you're doing). Milestone: **200k software users** (~$100k MRR, self-sustainable, organic growth).
  - Phase 2 — **Necklace (offline life)**: capture the world AI never sees: real life.
  - Phase 3 — **Glasses (real-time)**: "eyes beat ears for context by 10×"; AI sees what you see.
  - Phase 4 — **Brain (intent)**: intent before action; start with silent speech.
