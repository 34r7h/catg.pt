## 1. What it is

## 2. What it does
- **Replace the supply, don't just scan it:** rather than scanning for vulnerabilities after they have entered an environment, Echo replaces the supply itself — every artifact it delivers is screened for malicious code, patched against known vulnerabilities, and shipped as a drop-in replacement for what teams would otherwise pull directly from public registries, per Echo's own description.
- **AI agents that maintain the supply:** purpose-built AI agents research newly discovered vulnerabilities, identify affected software, find or develop a fix from unstructured sources, run compatibility testing, and open a pull request for human review, per Echo's own description.
- **Echo Frontier (generally available, October 2026):** for vulnerabilities found by frontier AI before public disclosure, Echo reorders the lifecycle to discover, validate, fix, protect, disclose, then converge upstream — discovering unknown flaws with model-driven analysis and human-led research, reproducing each finding against a working proof of concept, notifying maintainers and coordinating disclosure as an authorised CVE Numbering Authority, developing and backporting fixes with full-application compatibility testing, and delivering patched software privately through customers' existing JFrog or Nexus infrastructure, per Echo's own launch description.
- **Customers named in Echo's own material:** Varonis, EDB, Webflow, and UiPath, among others.
