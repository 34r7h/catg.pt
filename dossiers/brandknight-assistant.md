# BrandKnight — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**BrandKnight is a MAP (Minimum Advertised Price) violation monitoring tool for brands sold through resellers — currently in private beta with a waitlist.** It scans product listings across marketplaces around the clock, catches sellers who break your pricing policy, and produces timestamped evidence you can use for enforcement.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Brand / manufacturer: "24/7 MAP monitoring across 30+ marketplaces, with timestamped evidence instead of manual checks."
- Brand protection team: "Automated scanning + daily violation digests + analytics on repeat violators."
- Note: the site targets companies that sell through others (resellers/retailers); it is pre-launch — you can only join the waitlist.

## How it works (3 steps)
1. **Connect your products** — import your product catalog and define your MAP pricing policies.
2. **We monitor 24/7** — automated scanners check major marketplaces for pricing violations around the clock.
3. **Take action** — get timestamped evidence packages and enforce your pricing policy.

## Audiences and what each gets
### Brand / company (client)
- Continuous automated scanning of listings on Amazon, eBay, Walmart and 30+ marketplaces.
- Evidence capture: timestamped screenshots of every violation with full page context ("irrefutable proof for retailer conversations").
- Daily reports: a digest of all violations delivered to your inbox; shareable/exportable for enforcement.
- Analytics dashboard: trends, repeat violators, enforcement effectiveness.

### Brand protection / legal teams
- Multi-marketplace coverage in one place; enterprise security claimed: **SOC 2 compliant, encrypted data storage, SSO integration**.
