## 1. What it is
**Arrowhead is a Bengaluru-based enterprise voice-AI company that builds conversational voice agents for high-volume customer interactions, starting with financial services.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #17, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Official site:** arrowhead.ai.
- **Founders, per public coverage:** Devyani Gupta and Vengadanathan Srinivasan.

## 2. What it does
- **Voice agents that hold long, complex calls:** agents that handle end-to-end sales and service calls, often running up to 20 minutes, without customers realising they are speaking to an AI bot, per public coverage — explaining policy details, coverages, and exclusions, including from policy documents running to 20 pages.
- **Core financial-services segments:** lending (loan sales and collections), insurance (health, life, and motor sales and renewals), and securities (mutual funds and fixed-deposit sales), per public coverage.
- **Beyond financial services:** the company's own release describes use across customer support, sales, collections, insurance renewals, and other high-volume calling, with multilingual conversations and integrations with enterprise CRMs, diallers, and internal systems to complete approved actions during calls.
- **Design claim:** by specialising vertically in financial services, the bots replicate the behaviour of top-performing sales agents at scale, per the founders' public statements — treat conversion claims as company claims, not independently verified figures.
