# ShipSystems — distilled knowledge (from shipsystems.ai, crawled 2026-10-18; reviewed 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
ShipSystems is an AI engineering firm: they find where AI pays off in your enterprise, then build and ship scoped agents to production in your repo and cloud. Engagement-based approach (audit + build).

**One line per audience:**
- CTO/eng leadership: "Workflow audit to identify real AI opportunities, then ship scoped agents to production in your repo/cloud."
- Ops/product: "Find highest-value automations, build one scoped agent end-to-end, measure on real cases before going live."
- Enterprise teams: "Only pay for LLM where it earns its place; prefer deterministic (system one) where possible."

## Two-step engagement
- **Step 1 · Find** (2–3 weeks, fixed scope): audit operations/data/team; rank workflows by value/feasibility; produce prioritized roadmap with architecture + cost estimates. Credited toward first build if continue.
- **Step 2 · Build**: build one scoped agent end-to-end, measured on real cases before production. Phases: Prototype (working agent on your data, 2–3 wks), Evaluate (accuracy/cost/go/no-go, 1 wk), Production (shadow→supervised→autonomous, 2–4 wks), Operate (drift checks/tuning/runbook).

## How they think about LLMs
- Prefer **System one** (deterministic/rule-based selection) where possible: routing/classification/moderation/extraction checks/next-step choice; returns one of your options with probability; says "unsure" to escalate; fast/cheap (input tokens only).
- Use **System two** (LLM/chat) only where needed: drafting/summarizing/multi-step reasoning/explanation; free text output; slower/more costly (input+output).
- Mark each decision as system one or system two during Step 1.
