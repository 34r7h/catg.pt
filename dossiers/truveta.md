## 1. What it is
**Truveta is a health data and analytics company — the world's first health system-led data platform, with a vision of "Saving Lives with Data."** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #42.
- **Provider-led and governed:** owned and governed by 30 US health systems (as of 2025), formed in 2020 by Providence, Advocate Health, Tenet Healthcare, and Trinity Health, and publicly introduced in February 2021 with fourteen founding members.
- **What the data is:** de-identified electronic health record (EHR) data, daily-updated, for more than 130 million patients, sourced directly from member health systems — with full traceability to the clinical source — plus linked closed claims for 200 million+ patients across 100+ commercial payers, Medicare, and Medicaid, and mortality data.
- **What researchers do with it:** comparative effectiveness, burden of illness, health economics and outcomes research (HEOR), safety evaluations, and clinical trial support — tracking diagnoses, treatments, outcomes, and costs across settings, payers, and populations.
- **Truveta Studio and Truveta Data:** the analytics platform and data products for querying the dataset, including clinical notes at scale unlocked with expert-level AI, longitudinal imaging studies, and pediatric and mother-child data.
- **Truveta Language Model:** a multimodal large-language model introduced in 2023, designed to structure clinical data from EHRs.
- **Truveta Genome Project:** launched in 2025 with participating health systems, Regeneron, Illumina, and Microsoft.
- **Ethical guardrail:** the platform is licensed for ethical medical research, not for targeting advertising to patients or physicians; all data on the platform is de-identified.

## 2. How it is used
- Life sciences companies, member health systems' research teams, and public health agencies query de-identified EHR and claims data to answer research questions in days rather than years, per the company's positioning.
- Researchers study emerging therapies, capture early safety signals, and reduce reliance on costly trials and registries.
