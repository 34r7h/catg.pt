## 1. What it is
**Reflection AI is an AI research company building open-weight models and software agents for software development.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #50, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Asimov:** its first shipped product, a code-comprehension agent. Rather than writing code, Asimov helps engineers understand existing software — ingesting code, documentation, project notes, and team chat, and answering questions about how a system is built and why decisions were made, with a shared team memory layer.
- **Beam:** its first open-weight model, announced October 2026 per public coverage — a sparse mixture-of-experts model focused on coding, reasoning, and agentic workloads, with weights published for download and deployment on an organization's own infrastructure.
- **Positioning:** Reflection describes itself as a US open-weights AI lab, offering an alternative to both closed proprietary models and foreign open labs.

## 2. How it is used
- An engineer joining an unfamiliar codebase asks Asimov how components interact and why an architecture decision was made, before changing anything.
- A team captures senior engineers' institutional knowledge in Asimov Memories so it does not leave with departing staff.
- A team deploys Beam's open weights on its own infrastructure where data control and customization matter, rather than sending code to a third-party lab.
