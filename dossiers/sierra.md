## 1. What it is
**Sierra is an enterprise AI agent platform for customer experience, co-founded by Bret Taylor (former Salesforce co-CEO) and Clay Bavor (former Google executive).**
- **AI agents, not just FAQ bots:** Sierra agents answer customers and complete tasks on their behalf, such as upgrading a subscription or managing delivery, by connecting to enterprise systems.
- **Channels:** chat, voice, email, SMS, and WhatsApp, per public product descriptions.
- **Guardrails:** Sierra is described as addressing hallucinations by grounding agents in a company's policies and systems, with supervision and control for the business deploying it.
- **Customers cited in public reporting:** consumer brands including SiriusXM, Sonos, WeightWatchers, The North Face, Rivian, and ADT.

## 2. How it is used
- A company deploys Sierra agents as its customer-service layer, so customers get answers and task completion without navigating tools themselves.
- Agents use memory across interactions (Agent Data Platform, per 2026 reporting) and LiveAssist as a copilot for human call-center staff, suggesting answers in real time.
