## 1. What it is
**GoodSpace AI is an AI recruitment platform that automates finding, shortlisting, interviewing, and assessing candidates, and a career toolkit for job seekers.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #13, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.

## 2. What it does
- **For employers:** an AI-driven recruitment platform that finds and shortlists candidates, runs interviews and assessments, and integrates with existing applicant-tracking systems and career pages, per company announcements. Its stated pitch is cutting time-to-hire from an industry-standard 30 days to as little as 60 minutes in its own announcement — treat that as a GoodSpace claim, not an independently verified figure.
- **Multilingual AI interviewer:** a human-like AI interviewer that conducts interviews in multiple languages, per the company's launch announcement.
- **For job seekers (GoodSpace Gold):** an AI career toolkit including an ATS resume scanner, an AI resume builder, AI mock interview practice (behavioral, technical, and HR rounds), a cover letter generator, and HR contact tools, per goodspace.ai's own FAQ pages. Subscription prices for Gold are published on goodspace.ai and change — check the site before quoting a price.
