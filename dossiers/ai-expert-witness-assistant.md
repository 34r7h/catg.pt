## 2. When AI becomes central to a case (6 scenarios, site's framing)
1. **AI-generated evidence** — party claims audio/video/documents are AI-generated or synthetically manipulated; expert evaluates provenance, detection methodology, evidentiary reliability.
2. **Algorithmic decision disputes** — AI in hiring, lending or benefits decisions produces disputed outcomes; analysis of model design, training data, output patterns.
3. **AI product evaluation** — AI software fails in commercial or safety-critical context; whether specifications, representations, technical design were adequate.
4. **Generative AI and copyright** — training data, model outputs, boundaries of AI-assisted creation in IP disputes.
5. **Government AI systems** — public agency/contractor scrutiny over performance, procurement, deployment; specs, contract compliance, technical performance.
6. **Digital evidence authentication** — integrity, provenance, chain of custody of digitally processed evidence contested in civil or criminal proceedings.

## 6. FAQ (key answers)
- **What qualifies an AI expert witness?** — specialized knowledge (ML, algorithmic decision-making, digital forensics, AI-generated media); under **Federal Rule of Evidence 702** and analogous state standards, qualified by knowledge, skill, experience, training or education, applying reliable principles and methods.
- **Do experts testify?** — **Yes**: deposition testimony, trial testimony, arbitration; also expert report preparation, rebuttal analysis, consulting roles.
- **Timeline** — depends on matter complexity, technical domain, expert availability; conflict screening and initial technical review initiated promptly.
- **Civil and criminal?** — **Yes**, spans both.
- **Experts joining?** — prospective AI experts/researchers/specialists can submit credentials via the contact channel for review against active matter requirements.
