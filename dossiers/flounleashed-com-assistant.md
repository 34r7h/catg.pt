## Sizing, care and what's in the box
- Sizes (waist): **S 27–30" (youth athletes) · M 31–34" · L 35–38" (standard athletic build) · XL 39–42" · XXL 43–46".**
- Fit tips: measure at the narrowest point above the hips; measure in training attire; size up 1–2" for comfort; if between sizes, choose the larger.
- Care: wipe with a damp cloth after each use and air-dry; weekly mild soap and warm water; store in the carrying case, cool and dry, out of direct sunlight.
- **In the box:** FLO Unleashed belt (selected size), premium carrying case, quick start guide, care and maintenance instructions, **1-year warranty card.**
- The product page links a "How to Put On Your FLO Unleashed Belt" step-by-step video.
