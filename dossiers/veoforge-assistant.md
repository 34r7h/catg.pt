# Veoforge — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-18)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Veoforge** is an AI video platform that creates video ads from a product page or brief in minutes. It positions as "AI Video Ads From Your Product Page, in Minutes" — automated agent platform for going from idea to video with major models (including Seedance 2.5). It offers a free first video for qualifying companies (no credit card) and also does creative production/automation beyond video.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Performance marketers/brands: "Turn your product page into fresh video ads for Meta, TikTok, YouTube in ~12 minutes; iterate versions to fight creative fatigue."
- Agencies/in-house creative: "Brief it, approve script/storyboard first (no credits spent until approved), get finished brand videos back."
- Operations teams: "Beyond video, we automate repetitive ad/ops work (reports, data extraction, calls/questions) with AI."

## How it works
1. **Share** — Paste product page link or write brief; pulls product details, photos, brand colors.
2. **Approve plan** — Review script and storyboard scene by scene; no credits spent until you approve.
3. **Get ad** — Creates scenes, adds voiceover/captions, assembles; first version ~12 minutes.
4. **Run & iterate** — Use on Meta, TikTok, YouTube, product pages; make new versions when performance drops.

## Key features/claims
- First cut ~12 minutes; refine with version history and lip-synced voiceovers.
- Supports major models including Seedance 2.5 (stated in tagline/category).
- Free first video for qualifying companies, no credit card.
- You approve before anything is made (no spend until storyboard/script approved).
- Every ad is yours; can use uploaded presenters or product-only (no presenter).
- Use cases: performance video ads, digital twin videos (white-glove custom pricing), social media videos, SaaS/AI explainers, creative production partner.
- Also offers "beyond video" automation (ad reports, PDFs/invoices/bookings → spreadsheets, answering calls/questions) for select businesses.

## Positioning (common objections/solutions stated)
- Creative fatigue: make fresh versions when ads tire (creative fatigue checker tool linked).
- Cost/time of shoots: filmed ads $3k–$8k and take days/weeks; Veoforge ~12 minutes from product page.
- Need many versions for testing (hooks, lengths, sizes) without starting from scratch.
- No footage/product pages only: generate from product photos/page.
- No time to edit: handles script, VO, captions, assembly.
