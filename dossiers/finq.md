## 1. What it is
**FINQ is an AI-powered investment platform for self-directed investors that uses AI and big data to continuously analyze the market and give objective, data-driven investment recommendations.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — Israel #15.
- **Stocks DIY:** follow model portfolios designed for strategies like long, short, or market-neutral, or build a dynamic portfolio using relative stock rankings of the top 500 US stocks.
- **Pension optimization:** personalized portfolio recommendations and automated pension optimization, per the company's own descriptions; FINQ received a pension license from Israel's Capital Market Authority in March 2025, per third-party company intelligence.
- **AI-managed ETFs:** in February 2026 FINQ launched its first SEC-registered AI-managed US large-cap ETFs with autonomous stock selection, per third-party reporting — the FINQ FIRST US Large Cap AI-Managed Equity ETF (long positions in top-ranked equities) and the FINQ DOLLAR NEUTRAL US Large Cap AI-Managed Equity ETF (a long/short strategy), both applying a systematic AI-driven model that ranks S&P 500 stocks daily.
- **Core idea:** remove human bias and emotion from investing decisions by ranking stocks systematically rather than by analyst opinion.

## 2. How it is used
- An investor searches stocks in their portfolio to see how they rank, follows a model portfolio, or builds a dynamic portfolio from the rankings.
- US investors can also access the AI-managed ETFs through the usual brokerage channels for ETFs; this agent does not give investment advice and nothing here is a recommendation.
