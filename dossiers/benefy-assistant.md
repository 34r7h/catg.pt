# Benefy — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-18)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Benefy is a B2B flexible corporate benefits (benefits cafeteria) platform for Kazakhstan.** Instead of one identical social package for everyone, the company gives each employee a personal budget, and they choose benefits from the catalog themselves. HR does not collect receipts: budgets, limits, orders and reporting all live in the platform.

**One line for different audiences:**
- HR/business: "A benefits budget instead of a fixed package — plus full analytics without Excel."
- Employee: "You've been given a budget — you choose: gym, doctor, education, leisure."
- Partner: "Corporate clients with a budget, free to join, 20% commission on the sale."

## How it works (4 steps)
1. **Budget** — the company distributes budget to employees in tenge or points (the company sets the points rate itself; everything inside is in tenge).
2. **Choice** — the employee opens the catalog in the app or on the web and orders what they need.
3. **Service** — the partner receives the order, confirms it and delivers the service.
4. **Report** — HR sees spending, activity and demand in the analytics.

## Three audiences and what each gets

### HR / company (client)
- Budgets and limits by employees and departments — in one place.
- Spending and demand by category without manual consolidation (analytics: budget utilization, share of employees with orders, spending by category).
- Catalog and storefront customized to the company: the company selects available categories and benefits, and the storefront is styled in its brand colors — **two companies on the same platform see different catalogs**.
- Employee onboarding: self-registration via corporate email, bulk upload, manual adding; SSO; Excel exports.
- Where data is stored: **Kazakhstani cloud**, role-based access — employees' personal data is visible only to the HR of their company.
- Receipt-based reimbursement (a separate option): if a needed service is missing, the employee buys it themselves, uploads receipts, and gets money back within up to 14 days; recurring requests → the partner gets added to the catalog.
- Does not replace VHI/DMS: DMS remains the foundation, Benefy covers what a fixed package does not — the budget does not go to waste on those who don't need the benefit.

### Employee (user)
- Personal budget → catalog → order → partner confirms → service delivered.
- iOS/Android app (ru/kk/en): catalog, orders, balance, reimbursements, push notifications; a web cabinet is also available.
- Catalog is open without registration — you can look around in advance.

### Partner (service-provider business)
- Application on join.benefy.kz (~3 minutes, 3 steps: welcome → about the company → services and prices), then moderation and access to the cabinet.
- Benefits: ready-made demand (clients with a corporate budget), zero cost to join, self-service cabinet (services, prices, promotions), analytics by service and branch.
- Workflow: orders arrive in the cabinet / by email / on Telegram → confirmation → sales statistics.
- Already working with us: partner cabinet login — app.benefy.kz/cabinet/login.

## Positioning (objections from the site)
- **Against discount portals:** a promo-code list controls nothing after handout; Benefy is a full B2B cycle: company balance, personal employee budget, a confirmed order to the partner, analytics for HR.
- **Against "one identical social package":** usually HR chooses for everyone, receipts get lost in email and spreadsheets, demand is unknown; with Benefy each person takes what they want and the company sees real preferences.
- **About DMS:** not a replacement but an add-on (see above).
