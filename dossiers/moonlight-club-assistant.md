# Moonlight Club — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Moonlight Club is "a club for multi-hyphenate women building portfolio careers"** — a free Friday briefing (**The Memo**) plus quarterly, curated in-person evenings (**The Salons**) in New York and Philadelphia. "A gathering space for multi-hyphenate women building a portfolio career: **a name, an income, and options outside the 9 to 5.**"

**One line per audience:**
- Reader: "The Memo — free, every Friday, under five minutes, with one long read inside — plus a quarterly Salon you're invited to."
- Founder/partner: "Open newsletter, curated room: partnerships and press via tennessee@moonlightand.company."

## The two offerings
1. **The Salons (IRL)** — quarterly, in **New York and Philadelphia**. "A panel, a mixer, or curated seating. The two or three introductions you came for." Fine dining, thoughtful conversation, "intimate on purpose." **First Salon lands in New York this fall; invitations go to the founding thousand first.** Newsletter open to anyone, Salons curated — "who is at the table is the whole point."
2. **The Memo (URL)** — **every Friday, free, under five minutes**, "one long read inside." Topics: portfolio careers, positioning, and what women actually charge. "Every issue leaves you something usable: a **template, a rate benchmark, a prompt you can copy.**" Footer: "Unsubscribe anytime."

## Editorial DNA — "Three obsessions. Real numbers, real impact."
- **The Moonlight** — what you're building: portfolio careers, fractional consultancies, products, and the AI that helps you ship without quitting anything.
- **The Receipts** — the proof you know your stuff: positioning, speaking slots, board work, getting known without living online.
- **The Money** — the numbers nobody shares: what women actually charge, contract structures, the math of leaving corporate.

## Five standards (the site's manifesto)
1. The newsletter is open, the room is curated. 2. No gatekeeping — "If I know it, I write it down." 3. **AI-enabled, not AI-only** — it counts when it turns into income, hours saved, or work you can show. 4. Introductions over impressions — "Progress comes from proximity." 5. Real numbers, real impact — timelines, prices, trade-offs, and what it cost.

## Who it's for
- "You look successful. **You feel optionality-poor.**" — mid-career or mid-MBA; tech, consulting, finance, law, consumer, strategy; already building something else, or about to.
- Wants: real figures on what women charge, how fractional roles land, a small room of peers who have done it.
- Does NOT want: a handed-down plan, a career measured in followers, "a room where nobody says a real number out loud."
