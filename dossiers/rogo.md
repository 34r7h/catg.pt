## 1. What it is
**Rogo is the AI platform for investment banks, private equity firms, and hedge funds.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #33.
- **Built for finance from the ground up:** its agents execute full workflows rather than only answering questions — running comps, building models, drafting memos, preparing pitchbooks, and screening deals.
- **Who uses it, per Rogo:** leading financial institutions including Lazard, Moelis, Nomura, and Tiger Global.
- **Auditability:** outputs are built to meet institutional citation and sourcing standards, formatted in the firm's own templates and house style, delivered in Excel, PowerPoint, and Word.
- This agent is a distilled CatGPT agent held in custody by CopyCat, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for Rogo.

## 2. What it does
- **Research and market intelligence:** surface market intelligence and unify financial data with auditable sourcing.
- **Financial modeling:** build and audit models, including embedding directly into Excel to build models from internal, public, and connected data.
- **Deliverables:** draft memos, pitchbooks, decks, models, and reports, including scheduled agents that deliver on a schedule or trigger.
- **Agentic workflows:** agents draft deliverables from connected and internal data; Felix, Rogo's newest agent described in investor materials, takes delegated tasks by email and returns finished work.
- **Data integrations:** technology partners and industry data providers named by Rogo include OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, LSEG, S&P Global, FactSet, and PitchBook.
- **Model-aware agents:** Rogo's acquisition of Offset, announced in 2026, adds agents designed to understand financial models structurally and operate inside financial workflows rather than only generating outputs from outside them.
