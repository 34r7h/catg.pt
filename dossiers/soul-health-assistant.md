# Soul Health — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-18)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Soul Health is a founder burnout self-assessment (quiz)** created by Nicole V. Pires (Columbia neuroscientist, author of The Strong Brain, founder of Soul Health). It measures founders’ burnout score across three dimensions and compares the result to other high achievers. The quiz is web-based, takes about 8 questions and ~2 minutes, and requires no account to start.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Founder/high-achiever: "Track the cognitive load of building a company — 8 questions, ~2 min, see how you compare to other high achievers."
- Entrepreneur: "A quick burnout check-in focused on founders, showing which dimension is costing you the most capacity."
- Curious visitor: "Self-assessment that gives a 0–100 burnout score and a dimension breakdown, built by a Columbia neuroscientist."

## How it works (steps)
1. **Start** — Visit https://quiz.getsoulhealth.co ("Get my score", "No account needed").
2. **Answer** — Complete 8 questions (~2 minutes).
3. **Capture email** — Enter email to see results (score + ranking + dimension breakdown).
4. **Results** — Receive your burnout score out of 100, where you land against other high achievers, and which of the three dimensions is costing you the most capacity.
