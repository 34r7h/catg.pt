# Radius — distilled knowledge (App Store listing + radiusapp.co as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Radius is an IRL "meet-cute" dating app for singles in NYC.** It shows you who's single and physically in the same third space as you — café, bar, gym, park — in real time. You send a private like; if interest is mutual, you both know it before you walk over and say hi. The app ends where real life begins: no swiping, no bios, no messaging, no names.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Daters (users): "Know they're into you before even saying hi — in the places you already go."
- Press / partners: "Radius turns IRL attraction into real-time connection — bringing dating back into the wild."

## How it works (3 steps, from radiusapp.co + App Store)
1. **Spot someone in real life** — you notice someone cute while you're out in a third space (NYC).
2. **Open Radius** — see people in the same space as you in real time, find that person, send a like; your interest stays private unless they like you back.
3. **Know it's mutual** — match instantly, then go say hi in person, in the moment. If you don't act, the moment passes.

## What makes it different (App Store copy, verbatim essence)
- **No swiping. No bios. No messaging. No names.** Just one photo to recognise the person you're already looking at in real life.
- It only works when you're out in the world — **not on your couch**; you only see people physically sharing your space, not across the city.
- Positioning (blog): traditional apps removed friction by taking dating out of the real world; Radius removes friction **without taking dating out of real life**.

## Positioning / objections
- **"Another dating app?"** → it's not a chat product: no messages ever; the whole point is to get you off the app and into the conversation happening across the room.
- **"Why not just approach them myself?"** → you don't know if they're single or interested; Radius confirms mutual interest in real time and gives you the yes.
- Terms of Use (updated 2026-10-10): no matchmaking guarantees, no background checks — it facilitates introductions between adults physically present in supported spaces.
