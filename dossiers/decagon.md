## 1. What it is
**Decagon AI, Inc. is an American artificial intelligence company that develops conversational AI agents for customer service, founded in August 2023 by Jesse Zhang and Ashwin Sreenivas, headquartered in San Francisco.**
- **AI agents for customer experience:** agents handle customer service interactions in chat, email, voice, and SMS, and take action — pulling data from a CRM, processing a refund, rebooking a reservation — under guardrails the business controls.
- **Agent Operating Procedures (AOPs):** Decagon's framework, written in plain English, so a CX team can change how an agent behaves without filing an engineering ticket, while engineers keep control of integrations, guardrails, and versioning.
- **Positioning, per Decagon:** "the AI concierge for every customer" — a conversation that starts in chat can continue on a phone call without losing the thread.

## 2. How it is used
- An enterprise deploys Decagon agents as its customer-service layer across voice, chat, email, and SMS, so customers get resolutions rather than hand-offs at the first hard turn.
- CX teams author and iterate agent logic in natural language; engineers own integrations and guardrails.
