### Prompt engineering (lessons 7–8)
**A prompt** = the text/instruction you give AI. Better prompt → better output.

**Three prompt types:**
1. **Instruction prompt** — direct command with topic, audience, length, format, tone, language.
   - Weak: "write a YouTube script on financial tips."
   - Strong: "Write a 45-second YouTube script on financial tips for beginners, short sentences, friendly tone, strong hook at the start, in English."
2. **Role-based prompt** — "You are an expert digital marketer…" activates domain knowledge → professional output. Good for career guidance, sales/marketing, scripts, ads, proposals.
3. **Example-based prompt** — show a sample, AI copies style/tone/structure ("write a new paragraph in the same tone… about X").

**Five-part structure of a powerful prompt:**
1. **Role** — who AI should act as
2. **Task** — what you want
3. **Context** — details and background
4. **Format** — bullets, table, script
5. **Tone** — friendly, professional, funny

Example: "Act as a career coach and create a one-minute YouTube script in English explaining how students can write a strong resume using AI. Keep it motivational and use short sentences."

**Five prompt mistakes:** one-line prompt; no context; asking everything at once; not giving AI a role; not giving examples.

### Daily AI workflow — first hands-on task (lesson 10)
20–25 min/day system:
1. **Set 1–2 daily goals** (write one script, one post, learn one skill).
2. **Create a workspace** (Docs/Notion) with 4 sections: Ideas · Content Creation · Prompt Library · Daily Outputs.
3. **Prepare prompt categories**: idea generation, writing, research, editing, creativity.
4. **One mini task/day** (10–15 min): intro → headline → rewrite → idea list → simplify → product outline.
5. **Produce real outputs** (scripts, posts, covers, graphics, videos), not just chat text.
6. **Track progress** (date / task / output / time).
7. **Weekly reflection**: which prompts worked, where stuck, what to add.

Sample day: 3 min ideas (generate 10) → 10 min pick 1 + write with AI → 5 min edit → 2 min save best prompts → 5 min export/publish.

- Demo pattern: give raw paragraph → "rewrite this experience in a professional resume style with strong action verbs and measurable results."
- **Pricing ladder:**

### Data entry & virtual assistant work (lesson 13)
Pattern: **AI transforms/cleans/summarizes text → lightweight tool (Sheets/Airtable/Notion) as source/destination → human check.**
Demonstrated tasks:
- Generate sample datasets (ask ChatGPT for ~50 rows of fake contacts).
- Convert raw data → downloadable Excel/CSV.
- Extract structured fields from free text: "extract these fields… return as CSV rows with columns name, email, phone, city."
- Normalize: "normalize phone numbers to E.164 and addresses into street/city/state/zip, provide as JSON."
- Meeting notes → action items: "extract action items with owner and due date, use format: task, owner, due (YYYY-MM-DD), notes."
- Draft email replies: "short professional email reply to a client who asked for a delivery update, under six sentences, friendly tone, include ETA 3 business days, invite questions."
- Output formats available: CSV, JSON, Markdown tables — possibilities are limitless.

### Translations, summaries & notes (lesson 14)
- **Translation**: specify target language + tone (+ optional Roman transliteration). Use cases: blogs, YouTube scripts, Instagram posts, subtitles, ebooks, business docs. **$5–50/document.**
- **Summary**: "summarize this text in 5 bullet points, simple language" / "each point under 15 words." Sells: book summaries, research paper summaries, meeting summaries. **$10–100/job.**
- **Notes**: "create crisp revision notes from this text — bullet points, keywords, simple language" or advanced: "headings, subheadings, examples and mnemonics for fast memorization." Products: exam notes, class notes, mind maps, flashcards, cheat sheets — sell as monthly packages to students via Fiverr/Upwork/Telegram/WhatsApp/local.

### Ebooks with ChatGPT + Canva (lesson 16)
1. **Topic**: must solve a real problem. Prompt: "suggest 10 profitable e-book ideas for beginners in [niche]." Profitable niches: personal finance, weight loss/fitness, mental health, parenting, exam prep, digital marketing, cooking, self-improvement.
2. **Structure**: "create a detailed table of contents for an e-book on [topic] with 8–10 chapters."
3. **Write chapter-by-chapter**: "write chapter 1… simple language, add examples and tips" / "chapter 4 on budget planning, min 1000 words, friendly motivational tone."
4. **Value-adds**: intros, conclusions, action steps, checklists, worksheets, "7-day action plan…", affirmations.
5. **Edit and personalize** — pro tip for uniqueness/humanness.
6. **Design in Canva**: search "e-book templates," paste content, add headings/images/quotes/page numbers/cover.
7. **Title/cover**: "suggest 5 powerful titles…" / "create a subtitle…" — e.g. "Money Mastery for Beginners: A Simple Guide to Save Budget and Grow Your Income."
8. **Export PDF → sell**: Amazon, Kindle, Etsy, Gumroad, Payhip, Shopify, Instagram, WhatsApp. Price $5–50; offer basic/premium/bundle tiers.

### KDP books in minutes (lesson 18)
End-to-end ChatGPT-driven flow (~45 min writing, 3–4h to publish):
1. **Niche research**: "suggest topics on which I can write a book which sell most on Amazon KDP." Top sellers: self-help/personal growth, finance, parenting, AI, health & fitness, spirituality, low-content, children's stories. Pick per your strengths (demo: gut health & immunity).
2. **Title + subtitle** — ChatGPT proposes both (demo: "Heal Your Gut, Boost Immunity" / "A Simple Science-Backed Guide…").
3. **Outline/TOC** — generated with parts/chapters/sections.
4. **Set constraints**: "beginner-friendly, simple and easy to understand" → then just answer "yes" to continue chapter by chapter; give feedback or "try again" to rewrite.
5. **Copy-paste into Word** → .docx → upload to KDP.
6. **KDP metadata, all via ChatGPT:**
   - Description: "write a description of this book which motivates readers on KDP to read this book."
   - Keywords: "create KDP keywords" → pick ~7 from ~15 (broad + niche long-tail).
   - Categories: "what category can I place this book?" → 3 categories with recommended primary/secondary pair (demo: Health & Fitness → Nutrition → Diet Therapy + Diseases & Physical Ailments → Digestive Organs).
   - Author bio: "write about the author."

### Gemini storybook gem (lesson 20)
- Feature: **Gemini storybook gem** — generates story + illustrations (e.g. claymotion style) + studio-quality read-aloud voice.
- **Workflow**: prompt story → gem writes draft + illustrations → listen/download.
- **Repurpose**: screen-record with narration, or download images + ElevenLabs voiceover + video editor → YouTube videos.
- **Art styles**: clay motion, anime; for classics: watercolor, colored pencil, hand-drawn, pastel; modern: 3D, pixel, cartoon, flat vector; artistic: oil painting, abstract, pop art, sketch; moods: dark & moody / bright & cheerful / vintage.
- **Image-to-story**: upload photo (pet, toy, family) as main character; gem keeps character consistent.
- **Conversational edits**: regenerate, "make images look like comic book panels," "change the main character's outfit," "make it funnier."
- **Languages**: story text in 40+ languages (Spanish, Japanese, French, Hindi, Chinese, Arabic…). Best control: **English initial command**, then story can be any language.
- **Limits**: web images max 1024×1024 (square) — fine for digital, marginal for print; higher res via developer API; minor character inconsistency between pages; quality depends on prompt clarity; regenerate to refine.
- **Rights & money**: under Google's terms you own and may commercially use output, but **AI-generated images can't be copyrighted in the US** — you can sell, can't stop others reusing images. Gemini gem is a creation tool, not a sales platform.
- **KDP route**: generate content → save images + copy text → format in Canva at 8.5"×8.5" → upload PDF to Amazon KDP → Amazon prints/ships → royalty ≈ **60% minus printing cost**.
