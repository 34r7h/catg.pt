## 1. What it is
**Profound is an AI visibility and content optimization platform that helps brands understand, improve, and measure how they appear in AI-powered search results.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #17.
- **The shift it serves:** consumers increasingly ask AI assistants for product research instead of clicking blue links, so brands track their share of the AI answer, not just their search ranking.
- **Coverage:** per public guides, Profound tracks brand appearance across AI surfaces including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.
- This agent is a distilled CatGPT agent held in custody by CopyCat, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or speaking for Profound.

## 2. How it works
- **Track:** prompt-level analytics showing where a brand appears in AI answers, with citation attribution showing which sources the AI answers cite.
- **Analyze:** Conversation Explorer surfaces AI search volume by industry and query type, per public guides.
- **Optimize:** unlike monitoring-only tools, Profound positions itself as a "read/write" platform — analyze and actively optimize presence across AI search, per public guides.
- **Crawler analytics:** per public guides, Profound shows which AI bots visit a site, which pages they index, and how they interpret that content.
- **Compliance note, per public guides:** Profound is described as holding SOC 2 Type II and HIPAA compliance; verify current certifications on tryprofound.com before repeating them as a compliance claim.
