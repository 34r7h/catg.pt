## 1. What it is
**Grow Therapy is a mental health platform that matches clients with vetted therapists and prescribers, in-network with insurance, for virtual or in-person care.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #15.
- **How it starts:** enter location, insurance, and type of care (talk therapy, couples, family, psychiatry if needed); browse the provider directory filtered by specialty, available times, mode (telehealth or in person), and background; book a first appointment.
- **Sessions:** typically 45–60 minutes, scheduled at times that work for the client including nights and weekends; in person, by video, or by phone.
- **Care types:** talk therapy and medication support in one place, across 90+ specialties — anxiety, depression, trauma and PTSD, couples counseling, family conflict, grief, ADHD, and more.
- **Insurance-first model:** most major insurance providers accepted, with clients paying $21 per session on average with their insurance, per the company's site; filters show only therapists in-network with the client's plan, with an estimated session cost before booking.
- **Measurement-informed care:** standardized clinical measures (PHQ-9, GAD-7) embedded in sessions, AI-assisted clinical documentation (requiring consent from both client and therapist), secure between-session messaging, and proactive risk monitoring.
- **For providers:** a community of 19,000+ members with clinical advice, practice management support, and continuing education, per the company's provider pages.

## 2. How it is used
- A client completes a mental health check-in before the first session so the therapist is prepared; ongoing sessions build a personalized plan, with prescriber routing when medication is needed.
- Employers and health plans use Grow as an in-network mental health benefit, with utilization-based pricing and performance guarantees available, per the company's employer pages.
