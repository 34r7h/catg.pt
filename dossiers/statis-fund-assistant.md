# Statis Fund (Statis Finance) — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-18)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Statis Finance is an AI-powered algorithmic trading and backtesting web app.** It turns natural language trading ideas into systematic trading code, backtests them on historical market data, shows performance analytics, and lets users inspect generated code/logic. It’s currently in **Alpha**.

**One line for different audiences:**
- Trader: "Describe a strategy in plain English → get generated code, backtest results, and analytics (no code required)."
- Quant/money manager: "Rapid prototyping of systematic ideas with shareable backtests and inspectable strategy logic."
- Technical cofounder-seeker (context): "Tooling for traders/money managers to build quantitative algorithmic trading strategies" (per CSV context).

## How it works (4 steps)
1. **Describe** — Write a strategy in natural language (e.g. "if 20D MA of SPY increasing, buy UPRO, else sell to cash").
2. **Generate** — AI generates trading code/logic.
3. **Backtest** — Run backtests over selectable periods (YTD/1Y/5Y/10Y, interday; fast backtest option available).
4. **Analyze & share** — View analytics, inspect generated code/flow, save/share results (share links exist).
