# Kira World / Kira Labs — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Kira Labs** is an independent AI research lab in Newark, New Jersey, run by Robert McMurrer. Its flagship is **Kira World** — exploring synthetic people with continuing identity, reviewed memory, voice, relationships and place. Other projects include NewBrain (experimental trainable cognitive model), Patient Horizon (synthetic planning starter), ShiftBrief (free Windows app), Video Studio (private build), Companion Robotics research.

## How it works
- **Kira World**: Not a chatbot — aims for continuing person and place. Current prototypes include person-specific profiles, reviewed memory, bounded restart experiments, local voice work, basic walkable Home World shell. Future goals include digital homes, Notebook Worlds, accessible VR, compatible embodiment.
- **NewBrain**: Experimental trainable cognitive model; separate research project. Distinct systems (identity, reviewed memory, language model, emotional/world state) kept separate for inspectability; NewBrain explores source-traceable computational neuron models, recorded neural data comparisons, small engineered learning systems.
- **Other projects**: ShiftBrief (free Windows download for clearer team week), Video Studio (private build for local filmmaking), Patient Horizon (versioned records, synthetic planning starter, early R&D), Companion Robotics (research).
