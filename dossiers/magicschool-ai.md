## 1. What it is
**MagicSchool AI is an AI platform for educators and students — a toolkit of AI tools for lesson planning, assessment, feedback, and classroom engagement, built for education from the start, by educators.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #13.
- **80+ educator tools:** lesson plans, rubrics, quizzes, feedback prompts, IEP support, and more, in one account — a teacher moves from plan to rubric to quiz to feedback without opening a separate product for each step.
- **50+ student tools:** MagicSchool for Students, with teacher controls and Student Room Insights into engagement.
- **Raina:** an education-specific AI chatbot, alongside AI Slides generation and Adobe-powered image generation.
- **Built for schools:** outputs aligned to instructional frameworks and standards, an education layer that moderates content for age and school use, teacher visibility into student activity, and compliance positioning of FERPA, COPPA, GDPR, and SOC 2 Type 2, with student data never sold or used to train AI models, per the company's site.
- **District level:** leaders can build custom tools grounded in their own frameworks, share them across schools, set moderation rules, and use admin dashboards, rostering, and a signed data agreement on Enterprise plans.

## 2. How it is used
- A teacher creates a free account, picks a tool (for example, lesson planner), enters grade, subject, and standards, and edits the generated draft — outputs still need a teacher to check facts, reading level, and fit with the class.
- Districts run pilots with structured onboarding before committing to a full rollout, and can price Enterprise separately for admin controls.
