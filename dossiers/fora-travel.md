## 1. What it is
**Fora Travel is a platform powering a new generation of travel advisors / travel entrepreneurs.**
- **For advisors:** Fora gives advisors the technology, tools, training, and industry partnerships to build and grow their own travel businesses, including an Advisor Portal, on-demand and live training, content and marketing tools, and streamlined commission payments.
- **For travelers:** travelers get personalized trip planning, expert service, and VIP perks, booked through a Fora advisor.
- **Scale, per Fora's LinkedIn profile:** advisors have booked more than $3 billion in travel across 180+ countries; access to over 170,000 bookable hotels is cited in trade reporting, and exclusive partnerships are cited for upgrades and amenities at over 7,200 hotels and cruises.
- **Founded 2021, headquartered in New York,** per public profiles. Advisors join as independent contractors, per host-agency reviews — they do not need an LLC to sign up, per Fora's own answers in those reviews.

## 2. How it is used
- A traveler works with a Fora advisor for a personalized itinerary, hotel, cruise, or tour booking, rather than booking entirely self-serve.
- An advisor uses Fora's portal, training (660+ on-demand sessions cited in trade reporting), community forum, and partner perks to run their own advisory business.
