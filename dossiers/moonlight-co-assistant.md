# Moonlight & Co. — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Moonlight & Co. is a boutique consumer research and insights consultancy that is expert on exactly one customer: the "portfolio professional" — a senior person running more than one thing.** It runs the studies, the sessions and the partnerships that reach that customer, built on a standing panel of 800 with verified titles (the panel is **Moonlight Club**, a separate entity the firm researches and convenes on behalf of brands).

**One line for different audiences:**
- Brand: "We help brands win the customer who runs more than one thing."
- Founder/CMO without a team: brand and insights work you can run embedded — a fractional lead inside your team, three-month minimum.
- Investor: "One relationship, portfolio-wide reach" — a focused study, a convened room, or an embedded fractional brand lead for a portfolio company.

## How it works (the method, 3 steps)
1. **The panel exists first.** Moonlight Club: 800 senior professionals, titles verified, most women 26–42, each building something of their own. "It exists before the brief arrives, so we skip the recruitment stage that slows most qualitative work" (0 weeks of recruitment).
2. **Run the engagement** — one of three offers (below), designed, moderated and delivered by the firm.
3. **You get shippable output** — positioning language, a shortlist of editors/communities, or a brief; "the positioning language that came out of it."

## The three offers
- **Research and insights — "the Dossier":** 20–30 depth interviews with senior people who don't answer surveys, recruited from the panel and coded in house. Output: the positioning language that came out of it + a shortlist of the editors and communities who already reach them.
- **Sessions and dinners — "the Curated Convening":** a co-branded session built around one decision or one launch — a working session, a creator series, an executive dinner. "We design it, moderate it, and hand you the brief."
- **Owned audience partnerships — "the Map":** map the editors, communities and credible voices the customer actually trusts; build partnerships that feel organic because they are. For founders: brand and audience infrastructure your team runs without them. Explore: the **Culture Galaxy** (moonlightand.company/culturegalaxy/).
- **Embedded option:** any of this can run embedded — a part-time fractional lead inside your team, **three-month minimum**.

## The Culture Galaxy (the "Map" as a product)
A rigorously screened **map of independent Substack editors** with meaningful email reach — "the editors other editors recommend." Substack only; screened for mainstream brand partnership (it maps the commercially partnerable part of independent publishing, not cultural influence at large). Methodology as of 11 Aug 2026: 121,377 sitemap URLs → 12,489 with owned-audience evidence → 9,863 graph frame → 5,261 passing five screens → **2,147-publication final influence index** across 8 industries; 57 graph-elected anchors; tiers white/brass/dust (top 1% / next 19% / rest); **70** publications carry the curated ✦; 56 showcase publications (7 per industry). Curated by Tennessee Watt; independent research, not affiliated with or endorsed by Substack.
