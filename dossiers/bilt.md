## 1. What it is
**Bilt is a loyalty program that lets members earn points on housing payments — rent and mortgages — and redeem them for travel and other rewards.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #41.
- **The core idea:** housing is most people's largest monthly payment, and historically it earned no rewards. Bilt makes it earn.
- **How members pay:** through the Bilt app or the Bilt Payment Center; for landlords that do not accept cards, Bilt sends payment on the member's behalf, per Bilt's own materials.

## 2. How earning works, per bilt.com
- **Housing payments:** earn points on rent, mortgage, and HOA payments, with no transaction fee on those payments for Bilt cardholders, per bilt.com.
- **Bilt Cards:** three cards are offered on bilt.com — Blue (no annual fee), Obsidian, and Palladium — each with different everyday multipliers and benefits. Check bilt.com for current fees and multipliers, as card terms change.
- **Everyday earning:** multipliers on Bilt dining partners, hotels and flights booked through Bilt Travel, Lyft, and other everyday purchases, varying by card.
- **Bilt Cash:** a second currency usable at Bilt partner merchants (including Lyft, eligible hotel bookings through Bilt Travel, fitness studios, Grubhub credits, Walgreens credits, and GoPuff deliveries), and for transfer bonuses, status upgrades, member experiences, and point accelerators, per bilt.com. Bilt Cash expires — check current terms on bilt.com.

## 3. How redemption works, per bilt.com
- **Travel transfers:** Bilt Points transfer 1:1 to major airline and hotel programs, per Bilt's own materials.
- **Other redemptions:** rent credits, shopping, fitness, and a down payment on a home (through Fannie Mae and FHA programs, per Bilt's launch materials).
- **Neighborhood Benefits:** paying rent through Bilt unlocks benefits at a network of merchants — bonus points and complimentary items at local restaurants, fitness studios, rideshare, and pharmacies, per bilt.com.
- **Rent Day:** on the 1st of each month, Bilt drops limited-time benefits such as transfer bonuses, dining experiences, and chances to win free rent, per bilt.com.
