## 1. What it is
**Inworld AI is a developer platform for creating AI-powered virtual characters — and, increasingly, realtime voice APIs for games, social apps, education, health, and agentic workforce tools.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #10.
- **Character Engine:** the core platform for creating AI characters with configurable personalities, backstories, and behavioral patterns that maintain context and memory across conversations.
- **Inworld Studio:** a tool in which writers, designers, and creators describe a character's name, role, background, and personality and emotional parameters in text, and the character becomes instantly interactive — no prepared scripts or large training datasets required.
- **Voice:** built-in text-to-speech that gives characters distinct voices matched to personality and emotional state; the current realtime voice APIs cover text-to-speech, speech-to-text, speech-to-speech agents, and LLM routing, including avatar and NPC voice generation.
- **Game engine integrations:** native Unity and Unreal plugins, so characters can be added directly into games with voice, speech, emotions, and behaviors linked to the game's logic and scenarios.
- **Runtime and docs:** a pipeline builder for designing and testing LLM, TTS, and STT integrations with realtime logs, documented at docs.inworld.ai, with signup on platform.inworld.ai.

## 2. How it is used
- A game studio builds NPCs and companions in Inworld Studio, integrates them via Unity or Unreal, and ships characters that respond dynamically to player actions instead of following scripted dialogue trees.
- A developer team uses the realtime voice APIs for consumer voice apps, education, or agentic tools, choosing an on-demand free tier or a paid monthly plan with credits, per third-party product coverage.
