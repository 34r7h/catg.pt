# GovBridge — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**GovBridge is a Public Capital Architecture advisory for venture-backed and growth-stage companies.** It designs how a company integrates government procurement, grants and tax incentives into one coherent capital strategy — instead of chasing grants reactively. Tagline on the site: **"Engineering Public Capital as a Revenue Lever."** Selectivity is explicit: "We work with a limited number of companies each year. We architect capital. We do not chase it."

**One line for different audiences:**
- Startup exec: "We design your public-capital architecture — procurement, grants, tax credits sequenced correctly — before anything is filed."
- VC/portfolio ops: "Portfolio-ready public capital structuring with IP/investor alignment checked before applications."
- Someone seeking free grant money: "This is not a grant-matching service — engagement starts with a paid assessment and starts at $15k/month."

## How it works
1. **The problem it solves (site's framing):** opportunistic grant chasing, entering procurement without structure, misaligned capital sequencing, operational drag, risk exposure (grants accepted without structural review can compromise IP ownership and investor alignment).
2. **The model — three levers:** Revenue lever = procurement strategy (sole source / strategic partnership / competitive RFP / not pursued); Subsidy lever = structured grants & non-dilutive instruments (sequencing, IP protection, investor alignment evaluated before any application); Offset lever = R&D tax credits and investment incentives architected for real balance-sheet impact.
3. **Engagement funnel (5 steps):** Application (structured intake: capital stage, revenue, R&D spend, budget readiness) → Capital Fit Assessment (paid, time-boxed; "not a strategy session") → Diagnostic (comprehensive capital architecture analysis → roadmap) → Advisory (3–5 companies at a time, capacity-capped) → Execution (routed to specialized lanes when appropriate; strategy and execution remain separate).
4. **Qualification gate:** binary gate on stage and budget thresholds. "Applications are reviewed within 3 business days." "No advisory call is scheduled without a completed application. Calendly is not public. Budget readiness confirmation is required."

## Positioning / objections (site's own principles)
1. **Architecture precedes execution** — nothing is pursued/filed/bid until the capital architecture is designed.
2. **Procurement is a revenue lever**, not a bureaucratic exercise — they may advise against procurement entirely.
3. **Sequencing determines outcome** — order of instruments creates value or conflict.
4. **Scarcity is structural** — hard cap of 3–5 active engagements.
5. **Strategy and execution are separate disciplines.**
