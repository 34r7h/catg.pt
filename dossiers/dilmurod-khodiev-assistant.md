# Dilmurod "Dil" Khodiev — Distilled Profile

## Who / What (30s)
Dilmurod "Dil" Khodiev is a Tashkent-based operator-investor: Founder & CEO of C-Space (coworking network, since 2018) and Managing Partner of CS//Angels (private angel club, est. 2024). He built Tashkent's largest local coworking brand, then turned its founder pipeline into an angel-investment funnel — 2025 deployment $311,500, median check $36,000, ~50 member angels. Also advisor to the IRA Tashkent accelerator and a fixture on the Central Asia investment-forum circuit.

## Methodology & views (on record)
- Startup = "temporary organization searching for a scalable business model." [podcast 2026-04-30]
- Founder filter: commitment first; ability to sell second; failed founders often more fundable.
- Investor lens: MRR trajectory, team history, TAM. Warns on toxic investors.
- Startup Garage / C-Space pipeline accepts idea & MVP stage — earlier than most.
- Bullish on Uzbekistan to 2030; sees angel supply (not startup supply) as the bottleneck — hence the angels school.
- Operator-investor hybrid: still runs C-Space P&L while writing checks; community-first deal flow (coworking → pitch days → diligence → check).
Sources: Spot.uz 2026-02-10 & 2026-01-26; YouTube "Alohida mavzu" 2026-04-30; csangels.uz.

## How he works with founders
- Capital + mentorship, not capital alone ("supporting early-stage startups with mentorship and funding" — The Tech Media, Jan 2026).
- Pipeline is community-first: C-Space coworking → pitch days (Angel Pitch Day Jan 2026: 250+ applicants → 23 pitched → 10 selected → 13 to incubation) → club diligence → check.
- Accepts idea-stage and MVP-stage via C-Space Startup Garage — earlier than most accelerators.
- Accelerator work (IRA Tashkent) is revenue-and-deals focused, "not for lectures."
- Occasionally co-invests personally alongside the club (named on Screenix AI cap table).
Sources: csangels.uz, Spot.uz 2026-01-26, podcast 2026-04-30, Stanbase 2025-08-11.
