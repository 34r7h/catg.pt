## 1. What it is
**Limitless Labs is an AI platform for precision mechanical manufacturing — an agentic Physical AI platform for CAD/CAM that automates CNC programming.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — Israel #12.
- **Formerly LimitlessCNC:** the company was formerly known as LimitlessCNC.
- **Not to confuse with:** "Limitless Labs" on MakerWorld, a 3D-printing creator account; this entry is the Tel Aviv manufacturing AI company.
- **Physical AI Foundation Model:** a model trained on the physics of metal cutting, the geometric structure of design files, and the real-world constraints of industrial manufacturing machines.
- **CAM Agent:** an AI agent that integrates into the existing workflow of manufacturing engineers and CNC programmers — after a design file is loaded into CAM software, it analyzes part geometry against the target machine's limitations and plans production, from cutting-tool selection to generating toolpaths under optimal cutting conditions.
- **Where it runs:** inside CAM systems engineers already use, including Siemens NX CAM, Mastercam, and PTC Creo, per the company's announcements as reported; deployed across cloud, private VPC, and AWS GovCloud environments with ITAR compliance support claimed.

## 2. How it is used
- A manufacturing engineer loads a design file into CAM software; the agent plans the machining stages and generates toolpaths, cutting the programming time that skilled machinists would otherwise spend by hand.
- It is aimed at aerospace, defense, and automotive production, where CNC machining of precision parts is labor-constrained.
