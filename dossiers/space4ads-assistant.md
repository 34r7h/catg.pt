# Space4Ads — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-18)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Space4Ads is an out-of-home (OOH) advertising marketplace — described as "Airbnb for OOH".** It connects advertisers/brands with unique physical ad spaces listed by owners: vacant storefronts/walls, vehicles (vans, box trucks, semi-trailers, buses), mobile billboards, sports fields, venues, events, services/print shops. 

**One line for different audiences:**
- Advertiser/brand: "Find unique OOH inventory — storefronts, vehicles, mobile billboards, sports fields, venues, events."
- Space owner: "List your ad space and monetize unused space (vehicles, real estate, venues, events)."
- Agency: "Discover local OOH placements with photos, location, category, and owner details."

## How it works (4 steps)
1. **Discover** — Browse/search inventory by category/location (Vehicles, Real Estate, Venues, Events, Services, Sports Fields).
2. **List** — Space owners create listings with details/photos (https://www.space4ads.com/space/new).
3. **Connect** — Advertisers contact/connect with space owners to discuss terms/booking.
4. **Place/monetize** — Arrangements handled between parties (marketplace connection; specifics per listing).
