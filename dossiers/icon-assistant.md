# ICON — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**ICON (Icon New York) is a creative community-driven design and event studio based in New York City.** It curates culture through design-forward experiences and intentional partnerships, bridging fashion x design x tech. ICON produces events (mixers, brand activations, demos, panels, workshops) in NYC's creative spaces.

**One line per audience:**
- Brands/partners: "Partner with us for design-forward activations, panels and mixers that reach the right creative tech audience in NYC."
- Community: "Industry socials that connect fashion, design, tech, CPG, retail, media, PR, law and VC."
- Sponsors: "Curated IRL experiences for brands wanting to engage NYC's creative ecosystem."

## How it works
ICON focuses on **event curation and production**. From the site:
- Produces social mixers, brand activations, demos, panels and workshops tailored to partners' goals.
- Hosts events in iconic NYC venues and creative spaces (e.g. Citizen M Bowery, Fabrik Tribeca, Neuehouse Madison Square, Virgin Hotels, High Line).
- Bridges multiple industries: fashion, design, tech, retail+CPG, media, advertising, PR, law, VC and more.
- Publishes an event calendar on Luma (luma.com/icon.ny) and editorial via ICONology Substack (iconology.substack.com).
