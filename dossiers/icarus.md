Source: LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #40 (LinkedIn's own list entry, El Segundo, founded 2023); icarus.one via Y Combinator's company profile, accessed 2026-10-29. Pages skipped: none in this distillation — listing-plus-official-profile distillation, full site capture pending.

## 1. What it is
**Icarus develops autonomous solar-powered stratospheric aircraft for defense intelligence and connectivity.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #40.
- **The pitch, in Y Combinator's words:** building the modern-day U-2 spy plane — autonomous solar aircraft that fly at 60,000 feet for weeks at a time, eventually months and years, above weather and below satellites.
- **What that buys:** persistent intelligence and connectivity for the military at a fraction of the cost of existing systems, per Y Combinator's profile.
- **Founder, per LinkedIn:** Henry Kwan, a former NASA engineer who built satellites and designed and flew drones for NASA flight sciences.

## 2. The operating layer
- **The stratosphere as a layer:** above weather, below satellites — a position that avoids orbital-mechanics constraints while staying on station far longer than a conventional aircraft sortie.
- **Endurance over speed:** the value is persistence — weeks at a time on station — rather than rapid transit.
- **Dual use in one sentence:** intelligence (high-resolution observation from altitude) and connectivity (a high-altitude platform for communications), per public profiles.

## 3. Important distinction — do not confuse with Icarus Robotics
- **A different company, Icarus Robotics (icarusrobotics.com), builds autonomous robots for space stations,** including the Joy free-flying robot slated for ISS demonstration in 2027. It is not the LinkedIn #40 Icarus described here.
- **How to tell them apart:** the LinkedIn #40 Icarus flies stratospheric aircraft for defense (icarus.one, El Segundo, founded 2023); Icarus Robotics builds orbital labor robots (Brooklyn, founded 2024). Any claim about ISS robots, cargo stowage, or manipulator arms belongs to Icarus Robotics, not to this Icarus.
