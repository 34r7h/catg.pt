## 1. What it is
**Juicebox is an AI recruiting platform that automates candidate sourcing and outreach.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #39.
- **Mission, per Juicebox:** help teams win the talent war, on the premise that in the age of AI, human ingenuity is the scarce resource.
- **Who uses it, per Juicebox:** teams at Ramp, Notion, and leading AI labs, alongside thousands of customers from early-stage startups to Fortune 500 companies.

## 2. How it works, per juicebox.ai
- **Natural-language search:** type who you are looking for in plain language — no Boolean required — and Juicebox turns it into a high-precision AI recruiting search.
- **Scale of the pool, per juicebox.ai:** searches across 800 million candidate profiles aggregated from 30+ data sources, evaluating up to 5,000 profiles per search to surface the best match for an open role.
- **AI candidate summaries:** each profile includes a concise AI summary highlighting relevant skills, experience, and why the candidate matches the search.
- **Talent insights:** search results become a live view of the talent market — where candidates are located, most common skills, average tenure, and more — for recruitment planning.
- **Agents:** AI agents for recruiting that build searches through conversation (search calibration), assess every profile against custom criteria and company-wide preferences, and run sourcing, personalized outreach drafting, and multi-step follow-up sequences automatically, with tracking of opens, replies, and engagement.
- **PeopleGPT:** the natural-language candidate search tool named in LinkedIn's list entry.
- **Integrations:** candidate management and ATS integrations connect sourced candidates to the team's existing hiring process, per juicebox.ai.
