## 1. What it is
**FirstClub is a member-first quick-commerce platform in India selling curated, quality-tested groceries and everyday essentials, trading a few extra minutes of delivery time for stricter product standards.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #16, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.

## 2. How it is used
- A customer orders through the FirstClub app or site and receives curated groceries in around 30 minutes, per the site's own headline, or subscribes for planned morning deliveries.
- **Curation standard, per the site:** good groceries minus 200+ harmful ingredients — sweetness-tested fruit, preservative-free breads, palm-oil-free snacks, with items tested first and listed later, and every claim verified.
- **Categories, per the site:** fruits and vegetables, dairy and eggs, breads and bakery, staples, kids food and nutrition, packaged foods, snacks and beverages, health supplements, and cleaning essentials.
- **Fulfilment, per public coverage:** stores ("ClubHouses") in Bengaluru and Hyderabad, with regular lab tests on staples such as milk, atta, paneer, and dals before listing, and a membership model that had not yet launched at the time of the Series B coverage.
