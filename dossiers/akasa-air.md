## 1. What it is
**Akasa Air is an Indian low-cost airline headquartered in Mumbai, operating an all-Boeing 737 MAX fleet on domestic and international routes.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #20, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Official site:** akasaair.com; airline code QP, operating bases Bengaluru and Mumbai, per public profiles.
- **Founded 22 December 2021, commercial operations from 7 August 2022,** per public profiles, by a team including Vinay Dube and Aditya Ghosh.

## 2. How it is used
- **Booking:** flights booked on akasaair.com, the Akasa Air Android and iOS apps, or through travel agents and online travel agencies, per the airline's own releases.
- **Onboard experience, per the airline's own releases:** a brand-new fleet with ample legroom, USB ports on a majority of aircraft, and Café Akasa, its onboard meal service, including festive menus and options such as Kombucha.
