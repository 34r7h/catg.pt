# i18 — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**i18 (i18Dev, i18devs.com) is a B2B global-talent and GCC services company headquartered in Indore, India.** It helps companies hire global talent and build, manage and scale fully owned Global Capability Centers (GCCs)/captives in talent-rich hubs — mainly India — taking care end-to-end of Talent, Workspace, HR, Operations and Payroll. Positioning: "outcome-driven GCCs and global teams, built to scale from day one", with ownership and long-term impact instead of outsourced headcount.

**One line per audience:**
- Company/leader: "Hire Silicon-Valley-calibre talent at a fraction of the cost — or stand up your own GCC in weeks, not months, with no capex."
- Hiring manager: "Pre-vetted candidates in ~5 days, EOR and payroll handled, no upfront fees."
- GTM/revenue leader: "We build and run your profit teams — you pay when accounts close."

## How it works
**Three engagement models** (from the site):
1. **Managed talent and teams** — hire global talent: 5 steps: Discovery (role, experience, timezone, budget, start date) → custom strategy + i18 vetting (technical, communication, cultural fit) → interview (3–5 profiles shared with a fit matrix) → compliance, onboarding & integration (background checks, payroll, EOR) → value-added services (laptop/swag provision, monthly feedback check-ins, free replacement).
2. **Revenue teams as a service** (profit teams, no upfront capex) — 5 steps: initial discussion → target market mapping → generating leads (handover or i18 closes) → closing accounts → post-closure support and renewals. No charge until an account is closed.
3. **GCC as a service** (i18devs.com/global-2, fetched 19.10.2026) — Design (strategic workshops, GCC operating model, implementation playbook) → Setup (legal entity structuring & incorporation, facilities/workspace, talent sourcing) → Managed operations (HR/lifecycle, IT infrastructure & facilities ops, regulatory compliance, tax & accounting) on a **scalable subscription model, no capex or long-term commitments**.

**Engagement options within managed talent:** part-time contractor · full-time employee (i18 handles cross-border regulations, payroll, EOR) · direct-hire on the client's own payroll.
