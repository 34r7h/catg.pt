## 1. What it is
**Charlie Health is a virtual Intensive Outpatient Program (IOP) for mental health and substance use — more support than weekly therapy, without inpatient care.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #5.
- **Who it serves:** kids (ages 8–11), teens (12–17), young adults (18–34), and adults (18–64), with age-matched peer groups.
- **Program shape:** typically 6–12 weeks, 9–11 treatment hours per week — group sessions three times per week plus weekly individual and family sessions, with psychiatry optional — scheduled around school, work, and family life.
- **What it treats:** major depressive disorder, anxiety and panic disorders, PTSD and trauma-related conditions, substance use disorders, self-harm and suicidal thoughts, and co-occurring conditions.
- **Care team:** licensed therapists, board-certified psychiatrists and psychiatric mental health nurse practitioners, and group facilitators; modalities include DBT, CBT, compassion-focused therapy, motivational interviewing, trauma-focused CBT, and creative art and music therapy.
- **Coverage:** in-network with most major insurance plans, including Medicaid in select states, per the company's site; self-pay is available in select states.
- **Aftercare:** discharge planning to a weekly outpatient therapist, psychiatrist, or community resources, plus a free Alumni Program with facilitator-led groups and peer connection.

## 2. How it is used
- A family or adult starts with an admissions call and insurance verification, then joins a personalized schedule of group, individual, and family sessions from home.
- It works as a step-up from weekly therapy for someone seriously struggling, or a step-down after an ER visit, residential program, or inpatient stay. It does not provide detox — detox must happen before starting care, per the company's site.
