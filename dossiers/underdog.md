## 1. What it is
**Underdog — full name Underdog Fantasy, now branded Underdog Sports — is a sports gaming and media company built for American sports fans.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2025 — US #3.
- **Daily fantasy sports (DFS):** the core product — snake drafts and Best Ball tournaments, where players draft teams and compete over a season or tournament without managing lineups weekly.
- **Pick'em:** games in which players pick over/under on athlete performance metrics (points, rebounds, assists, total yards) against the house's lines.
- **Prediction markets and sports betting:** an emerging sports betting platform and a prediction-market product, added as the company expanded beyond fantasy; availability varies sharply by state and is the subject of ongoing legal disputes in some states.
- **Media network:** a sports media network that includes daily sports programming and creator partnerships.
- **Apps:** iPhone and Android apps, renamed "Underdog: Sports, Games & Rips" as products were added, per 2026 reviews.

## 2. How it is used
- A player enters a draft or Best Ball tournament, or plays Pick'em on athlete stat lines, and follows entries live in the app.
- Availability, contest types, and promotions differ by state — for example, Pick'em is unavailable in New York, and drafts were shut down in seven states in September 2026 to protect the prediction-market platform, per 2026 reviews. Check the current app and state rules before playing.
