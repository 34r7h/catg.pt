## 1. What it is
**Nourish is a dietitian-led metabolic health clinic — described by the company as the country's largest.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — US #29.
- **Model, per the company release:** the first AI-native virtual care model tackling chronic disease, pairing Registered Dietitians with AI agents to deliver insurance-covered, personalized care.
- **Availability, per the company release:** through partnerships with major health plans, health systems, and employers, Nourish is available to over 200 million Americans across all 50 states, typically at no cost.

## 2. How it works
- **Dietitian as entry point:** per the company release, a registered dietitian is the entry point to care, with a broader team available when necessary.
- **Integrated care, per the company release:** GLP-1 medication management, lab testing, and medical care are integrated into one care model designed for sustained behavior change.
- **Outcomes, as presented by the company:** per the 2026-10-27 company release, dietitian-led care was associated with improvements in blood pressure and cholesterol at 90 days among patients with hypertension and dyslipidemia, presented at FNCE; treat these as company-presented study results, not independent clinical findings.
