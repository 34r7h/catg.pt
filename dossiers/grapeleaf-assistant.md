# Grapeleaf — distilled knowledge (from the website as of 2026-10-19)

## What it is (30 seconds)
**Grapeleaf is a fundraising platform where founders record video pitches in three lengths — one, three and five minutes — and investors watch only what they asked to see.** Tagline: **"1 minute starts the conversation."** Pitch: *"The warm intro, without the introduction."* Status: **early access; joining the waitlist is free**; video pitches and paid memberships are "coming next" (site, as of 2026-10-18).

**One line for different audiences:**
- Founder: "Publish three cuts of your pitch (1/3/5 min); verified investors who match your sector and stage watch — and you see exactly where they drop off."
- Investor/scout: "Filtered deal flow you chose, anonymous until you decide, zero inbound — 15 minutes of pitches a day, one email, Monday."
- Affiliate: "Your referral link earns 50% of a member's first eligible payment, up to $500."

## How it works (the funnel)
1. **1 Minute — The hook.** Company, sector, stage, one line. If an investor skips, the founder is never told — "silence is the default, not rejection."
2. **3 Minutes — The business.** ARR, growth, team, what you're raising. Unlocked only by a thumbs up ("Keep") on minute one.
3. **5 Minutes — The deep dive.** Then — and only then — the deck, public Q&A, LinkedIns, email. "Nine minutes in," the investor decides whether to say hello.
4. **Grapeleaf steps out at hello.** Direct contact (email, calendar). No broker, no chat, no success fee.
- Anonymous Q&A: investors ask as "Investor A/C"; a founder's answer is "answered once, visible to every investor watching."
- Illustrative demo data on the site (example startup "Vinemark": fintech, Seed, Lisbon, ARR €1.4M, raising €6M Series A; weekly stats "Saw your 1 min 312 · Watched it all 194 · 3 min 61 · 5 min 19 · Unlocked you 7", Aug 18–25 example) — treat all in-page numbers as product examples, not real metrics.

## Per audience

### Investors & scouts
- Filters: sector, stage, geography, cheque size — change any time; example "Fintech · Seed · Europe · 15 min/day".
- **Anonymity:** "Investor A" until you choose to introduce yourself — never before.
- **Quiet by design:** one email, Monday (weekly digest of new founders matching the filter). "No mid-week pings, no founder DMs, no 'just circling back'."
- **Zero inbound:** a founder can never reach your inbox on Grapeleaf.
- Membership **will require verification**: fund or angel, work email, one proof point (portfolio page, Crunchbase, or a vouch from a member).
- Example session: 62 founders match → watch a minute each → keep six → two earn the full nine → introduce yourself to one.

### Founders & operators (pre-seed to Series A)
- Three cuts always live; every matching investor sees minute one; the rest they earn.
- Funnel analytics: views, completion, **drop-off timestamps** ("Most investors leave your 3 min at 1:48 — right after the pricing slide. Recut that minute.").
- Control: block named funds and competitors' investors; pause profile in one tap; contact details locked until someone finishes all nine minutes.
- Membership includes: three pitch videos always live, full funnel & drop-off analytics, public Q&A replies, one company per account ("no agents, no spam").

### Affiliates (anyone)
- Free affiliate account (no waitlist registration or membership required); personal link/code; first eligible introduction keeps credit; self-referrals don't qualify; code "GRAPELEAF" is a general invitation with no credit.
- Commission table (site examples at 50%): Season $350→$175 · Founder annual $1,000→$500 · Founder monthly $150→$75 · Investor annual $1,000→$500 · Investor monthly $150→$75. Payout setup comes with the paid membership program.

## Positioning (three rules the site "won't trade away")
1. **Investors initiate, or nothing happens** — that constraint is why investors show up at all.
2. **Every investor is verified** — fund or angel, work email, one proof point; "founders are handing over nine minutes; the floor has to be real."
3. **A skip is silent** — no rejection theatre, no scores, no leaderboards.
- vs cold outreach/forwarded decks: "No warm-intro scavenger hunt, no forwarded deck dying in a thread."
