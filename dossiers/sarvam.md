## 1. What it is
**Sarvam AI is an Indian AI company building large language, speech, translation, and document models trained for Indian languages.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — India #9, on LinkedIn's measures of employee growth, engagement, job interest, and attraction of top talent.
- **Built for Indian languages first:** models are trained and evaluated on Indian languages, accents, and code-mixed usage, not adapted from an English-first baseline, per Sarvam's own docs.
- **Model family, per Sarvam's docs:** Saaras speech-to-text across 23 languages (22 Indian + English); Bulbul text-to-speech across 11 languages (10 Indian + English); Mayura translation between 11 languages with context preservation; Sarvam Vision document intelligence with OCR and structured output across 23 languages; and the Sarvam-105B flagship chat model.
- **Indus:** Sarvam's consumer chat app, powered by its models, positioned as an Indian-language-first assistant.
- **Sovereign-AI role:** Sarvam was selected under India's IndiaAI Mission to build a foundational model, per public coverage — its models are described as trained on domestic Indian infrastructure.

## 2. How it is used
- A developer drops Sarvam into existing frameworks and telephony via its APIs for speech, text, translation, and document work.
- A business builds a Hindi, Tamil, or other Indian-language support chatbot, voice agent, or document-translation flow that handles code-mixed input.
- A team uses Sarvam Vision to extract structured data from multi-script Indian documents that English-first OCR systems routinely misread.
