## 1. What it is
**Encore AI is an agentic AI platform that analyzes customer interactions to train sales and service agents, turning top-performer behavior into revenue agents.** It is listed as LinkedIn Top Startups 2026 — Israel #11.
- **Interaction Mining:** patented technology that extracts top-performer behaviors from interaction data — calls, chats, emails, and CRM data.
- **Autonomous AI agents:** agents built from those behaviors that convert leads, close applications, and drive upsell, deployed across voice, chat, IVR, and live form-fill channels.
- **Who it serves:** banks, insurers, healthcare providers, and other organizations where customer interactions directly affect business performance.
- **Compliance architecture:** built for regulated industries including financial services, per the company's own product descriptions as reported.
- **Deployment:** the company claims go-live in weeks, not months, ingesting interaction data without months of configuration — a company claim, not a verified outcome.

## 2. How it is used
- An enterprise feeds Encore its interaction data; Interaction Mining finds what top performers do differently; Encore deploys agents that apply those behaviors in live customer conversations.
- Teams use it where sales enablement previously relied on manual call reviews and coaching.
