## 1. What it is
**ElevenLabs is an AI audio research and deployment company, best known for realistic, versatile, contextually-aware speech.**
- **Text-to-Speech:** type text and generate natural-sounding speech in 70+ languages, per elevenlabs.io summaries, with pre-made voices and custom voice design.
- **Voice Cloning:** Instant Voice Cloning from a short sample, and Professional Voice Cloning from longer recordings, with verification that the voice is your own for professional clones.
- **Dubbing Studio:** translate and dub audio and video into other languages while attempting to preserve the original speaker's voice characteristics.
- **Voice Library:** a marketplace to share and discover community voices.
- **Other audio:** sound effects, music generation, speech-to-text, and conversational voice agents, per public guides.

## 2. How it is used
- Voice audiobooks, news articles, video game characters, film pre-production, localization, social and advertising audio, and accessibility uses, per the company's own description.
- For creators: generate voiceovers in a browser or via API, then export audio for video or podcast work.
- For developers: use the API for text-to-speech, speech-to-text, dubbing, and voice agents.
